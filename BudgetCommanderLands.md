## 🧭 1. Core Philosophy & Selection Criteria

Building an effective Commander mana base on a budget does **not** require $400 fetch/shock/ABUR dual suites. Instead, success relies on **tempo management** and **sequencing**.

### The Two Golden Rules
1. **Untapped Potential is King:** Prioritize lands that enter the battlefield untapped unconditionally or have easily satisfied conditions in the first 3 turns (Pain lands, Check lands, Tango lands, Fast lands, Filter lands).
2. **Upside if Tapped:** If a land *must* enter the battlefield tapped, it must provide meaningful compensation:
   - **Type Synergy:** Possesses basic land types (Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, Forest) to enable land tutors (*Farseek*, *Nature's Lore*, *Three Visits*, *Skyshroud Claim*) and Check lands.
   - **Card Velocity:** Low-cost cycling (Bicycle/Cycling lands) or card selection (Scry lands / Temples).
   - **Late-Game Value:** Animating into an evasive creature (Restless creature lands) or ramp/fixing utility (Bounce lands, Landscapes).

### What to Omit (The "Bulk Tapped Land" Trap)
Unless your commander directly synergizes with them, **avoid spending money or deck slots** on low-impact tapped duals:
- ❌ **Common Gain Lands** (e.g., *Jungle Hollow*, *Tranquil Cove*): 1 life does not justify losing an entire turn of tempo.
- ❌ **Common Indestructible Artifact Duals / Bridges** (e.g., *Rustvale Bridge*): Only run if your deck specifically leverages Artifact count, Affinity, or mass land wipe combos.
- ❌ **Basic Guildgates** (e.g., *Orzhov Guildgate*): Omit unless running a dedicated Gates engine (*Maze's End*, *Nine-Fingered Keene*).
- ❌ **Vanilla Common Draft Duals** (e.g., *Molten Tributary*, *Elvenking's Halls*): While typed, they always enter tapped with zero additional upside. Use them only if you already own them and lack proper alternatives.

## 🎨 3. Two-Color Guild Lands (Under $2)

The 10 two-color guild combinations broken down by their most effective budget cycles:
- **Pain Lands:** Always enter untapped; tap for {C} for free, or {C1}/{C2} for 1 life (negligible in a 40-life format).
- **Check Lands (Buddy Lands):** Enter untapped if you control a land with either corresponding basic type.
- **Tango Lands (Battle Lands):** Enter untapped if you control 2+ basic lands; **have both basic land types**.
- **Cycling Duals (Bicycle Lands):** Have both basic land types; enter tapped but cycle for {2} when flooded.
- **Odyssey Filter Lands:** Enter untapped; convert {1} into two colors in any combination.
- **Shadowmoor / Eventide Filter Lands:** Enter untapped; convert hybrid mana into double colored mana.
- **Fast Lands:** Enter untapped on Turns 1–3 (two or fewer other lands); premier early-game acceleration.
- **Reveal Lands (Snarls):** Enter untapped if you reveal a basic of either type from hand.
- **Scry Lands (Temples):** Enter tapped, but smooth opening hands and topdecks with Scry 1.
- **Restless Creature Lands:** Enter tapped, but animate in the mid/late game into impactful combat threats with attack triggers.

## 🔍 5. Budget Land Fetches (Under $2)

Budget fixing that extracts lands directly from your deck to fix colors, thin the deck, or trigger Landfall:

### Mirage "Slow Fetches" (Typed Dual & Triome Fetches Under $1)
> 💡 **The Budget Secret:** Unlike *Evolving Wilds*, the 5 allied Mirage fetches search for **basic land types** (Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, Forest)—meaning they **can tutor Tango lands, Cycling duals, Snow duals, DMU common duals, and even Shock lands or Triomes** on a budget. While the fetch itself enters tapped, the land it tutors **enters untapped**!
*   [**Flood Plain**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Flood+Plain") (Azorius) — Fetches Plains or Island.
*   [**Bad River**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bad+River") (Dimir) — Fetches Island or Swamp.
*   [**Rocky Tar Pit**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Rocky+Tar+Pit") (Rakdos) — Fetches Swamp or Mountain.
*   [**Mountain Valley**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mountain+Valley") (Gruul) — Fetches Mountain or Forest.
*   [**Grasslands**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Grasslands") (Selesnya) — Fetches Forest or Plains.

### The Budget Super-Ramp Fetch
*   [**Krosan Verge**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Krosan+Verge") (~$0.30) — Enters tapped; taps for {C}. `{2}, {T}, Sacrifice`: Search for **a Forest card AND a Plains card** (including typed nonbasic duals/triomes) and put them onto the battlefield tapped. Two lands ramped from one card!

### Universal Basic Land Fetches

## 📋 12. Quick Deckbuilder Land Package Cheatsheet

When assembling the mana base for a new deck, use this formula to quickly select cards from this guide:

### For Two-Color Decks (e.g., Selesnya, Dimir)
1. **Staples (3):** *Command Tower*, *Exotic Orchard*, *Path of Ancestry*.
2. **Untapped Guild Duals (5–7):** Pain Land (*Brushland*), Check Land (*Sunpetal Grove*), Tango Land (*Canopy Vista*), Odyssey Filter (*Sungrass Prairie*), Fast Land (*Razorverge Thicket*), Reveal Land (*Fortified Village*).
3. **Card Advantage / Cycling (2–3):** Cycling Land (*Scattered Groves*), Scry Land (*Temple of Plenty*), Restless Creature Land (*Restless Prairie*).
4. **Basic Land Fetches (2–4):** *Fabled Passage*, *Ash Barrens*, *Escape Tunnel*, *Myriad Landscape*.
5. **Utility / MDFC Lands (3–5):** *Bonders' Enclave*, *Rogue's Passage*, *Demolition Field*, 1–2 MDFC spell-lands.
6. **Basic Lands (18–22):** Split according to your deck's colored mana pip distribution.

### For Three-Color Decks (e.g., Jund, Esper, Naya)
1. **Staples (4):** *Command Tower*, *Exotic Orchard*, *Path of Ancestry*, *Fabled Passage*.
2. **Three-Color Lands (2):** Matching MH3 Landscape (e.g., *Twisted Landscape*) + Tri-Land (e.g., *Savage Lands*).
3. **Guild Duals (3 per guild pair = 9 total):**
   - 3 Pain Lands (*Sulfurous Springs*, *Karplusan Forest*, *Llanowar Wastes*)
   - 3 Check Lands (*Dragonskull Summit*, *Rootbound Crag*, *Woodland Cemetery*)
   - 3 Tango Lands (*Smoldering Marsh*, *Cinder Glade*, *Vernal Fen*)
4. **Filters & Bounces (3–4):** Best Odyssey/Eventide Filters or Karoo bounce lands.
5. **Basic Fetches (3–4):** *Ash Barrens*, *Promising Vein*, *Evolving Wilds*, *Myriad Landscape*.
6. **Utility / MDFC Lands (3–4):** *Demolition Field*, *Bojuka Bog*, 1–2 colored utility/MDFCs.
7. **Basic Lands (12–16):** Ensure enough basic types to reliably turn on your Tango and Check lands.
