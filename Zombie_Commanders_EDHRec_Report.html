TOP PICK • THE PERFECT SOLUTION

Marneus Calgar ({2}{W}{U}{B})

W,U,B (Esper) • Legendary Creature — Astartes Warrior • 4/5 • Double strike
Tier S — Dual Engine (Card Draw + Token Spigot)
Why This Solves Your Playtest Frustration

Varina required attacking with creatures just to loot (filtering cards without increasing hand size), leaving you empty-handed after removal. Marneus Calgar states: 'Whenever one or more tokens enter the battlefield under your control, draw a card.' Every single Zombie token created across the deck (from Field of the Dead, Endless Ranks of the Dead, Cryptbreaker, Jadar, Headless Rider, Diregraf Colossus, or Tombstone Stairwell on EVERY player's upkeep) automatically draws raw cards to refill your hand. Plus, you retain the entire Esper pool (White token doublers, Blue counterspells and flying evasion).

General Strategy & Playstyle

Flood the board with repeatable, low-cost Zombie token generators. Each token trigger fuels Marneus's card draw engine, rapidly drawing into anthems, token doublers (Anointed Procession, Mondrak), and game-ending overrun spells. If an opponent wipes your board, your hand is already packed with 7 cards to rebuild instantly. Has a built-in {6} mana sink to create two 2/2 tokens in top-deck mode.

Example Win Conditions (Bracket 3 Focus)
  • Akroma's Will: Double strike, flying, lifelink, indestructible, and protection from all colors on a wide Zombie army.
  • Tombstone Stairwell Overrun: Drops hasted 2/2 Tombspawn on every upkeep, drawing 4 cards per turn cycle with Marneus while overwhelming opponents with Wonder flying.
  • Zombie Master + Urborg: Renders your entire token horde completely unblockable with universal swampwalk.
