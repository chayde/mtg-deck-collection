# Fetchable Lands & Commander Duals Reference Guide

> **Scope:** Commander / EDH Format Exclusively  
> **Last Updated:** September 2026  
> **Purpose:** An authoritative, tiered reference guide ranking fetch lands, original Revised duals, multiplayer crowd lands, and all fetchable dual/tri-land cycles. Categorized strictly by tempo efficiency, entering untapped status, drawbacks, and keyword utility.

---

## 🧭 1. Commander Mana Philosophy & Tiering Logic

In Commander, mana efficiency and color consistency form the backbone of every deck. While basic lands provide stability against nonbasic hate (*Blood Moon*, *Back to Basics*, *Wave of Vitriol*), running lands with **basic land types** (Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, Forest) fundamentally unlocks:

1. **The 10 Fetch Lands:** Turning any fetch land into a multi-color rainbow fixer.
2. **Type-Specific Land Ramping:** Enabling premier ramp spells like [**Nature's Lore**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nature's+Lore"), [**Three Visits**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Three+Visits"), [**Farseek**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Farseek"), and [**Skyshroud Claim**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Skyshroud+Claim") to fetch untapped duals.
3. **Check Land Enablement:** Turning on Check lands ([**Drowned Catacomb**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Drowned+Catacomb"), [**Sulfur Falls**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sulfur+Falls"), etc.) from Turn 1 or 2 onwards.

### Ranking Methodology
1. **The Engines:** The 10 Fetch Lands themselves.
2. **Tier 1 (Unconditional Untapped Duals):** Original Revised duals (zero drawback).
3. **Tier 2 (Multiplayer Untapped Duals):** Crowd lands (unconditional untapped in 3+ player games; *note: non-fetchable, but included here as the gold standard for Commander tempo*).
4. **Tier 3 (Payoff-for-Life Untapped):** Shock lands (2 life is negligible in a 40-life format).
5. **Tier 4 (Condition-Dependent Untapped):** Battle / Tango lands (require 2+ basic lands).
6. **Tier 5 (Enters Tapped with Premier Card Selection):** Surveil lands (Surveil 1 smooths draws and fuels graveyards).
7. **Tier 6 (Enters Tapped with 3 Colors & Cycling):** Triomes & New Capenna Tri-Lands (3 types, Cycling {3}).
8. **Tier 7 (Enters Tapped with Cycling):** Bicycle lands (Cycling {2}).
9. **Tier 8 (Enters Tapped with Typal/Niche Synergy):** Kaldheim Snow Duals (Snow supertype).
10. **Tier 9 (Enters Tapped with No Inherent Upside):** Dominaria United Common Duals (budget Domain enablers).
11. **Tier 10 (Specialty Utility Fetchable Lands):** Monocolored utility lands (*Mystic Sanctuary*, *Witch's Cottage*) and *Murmuring Bosk*.

### Engines: The 10 Fetch Lands
*Pay 1 life, tap, sacrifice: Search your library for a Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, or Forest card, put it onto the battlefield, then shuffle.*

Because they search for a **land type** rather than a "basic land", each fetch land can find any typed dual, triome, shock, or utility land matching either of its designated types.

### Tier 2: Multiplayer "Crowd Lands" / Bond Lands
*Enters the battlefield tapped unless you have two or more opponents. In standard 4-player Commander games, these are 100% unconditional untapped dual lands.*

> ⚠️ **Fetchability Notice:** Crowd lands do **not** have basic land types and cannot be fetched via fetch lands or *Farseek*/*Nature's Lore*. However, for multiplayer Commander exclusively, their zero-drawback untapped speed places them directly behind Revised duals for turn-by-turn tempo.

### Tier 3: Shock Lands (Ravnica)
*As this land enters the battlefield, you may pay 2 life. If you don't, it enters the battlefield tapped. Possesses both basic land types and is fully fetchable.*

In Commander's 40-life format, paying 2 life is a trivial cost to access untapped multi-color mana on Turn 1, 2, or 3. They are the premier fetchable duals in modern Magic.

### Tier 4: Battle Lands / "Tango Lands" (Battle for Zendikar)
*Enters the battlefield tapped unless you control two or more basic lands. Possesses both basic land types and is fully fetchable.*

* **Downside:** Always enters tapped on Turn 1 and Turn 2.
* **Upside:** Enters untapped from Turn 3 onwards if your deck runs a healthy basic land suite (~12–18 basics). Easily fetched untapped with *Nature's Lore*, *Three Visits*, or fetch lands in the mid-to-late game.
* **Cycle Status:** Currently only exists for the **5 allied color pairs**.

* [**Prairie Stream**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Prairie+Stream") — Land — Plains Island ({W}/{U})
* [**Sunken Hollow**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sunken+Hollow") — Land — Island Swamp ({U}/{B})

### Tier 5: Surveil Lands (Murders at Karlov Manor)
*Enters the battlefield tapped. When this land enters the battlefield, surveil 1 (look at the top card of your library; you may put it into your graveyard). Possesses both basic land types and is fully fetchable.*

* **Why They Rank Above Other Tapped Duals:** At the end of an opponent's turn, cracking a fetch land to tutor a Surveil land guarantees card selection, smooths opening draws, or bins reanimation targets (*Living Death*, *Meren*, *Henzie*, *Chainer*) at zero card cost.

### Tier 6: Fetchable Triomes & New Capenna Tri-Lands

### Tier 10: Specialty Fetchable Utility Lands
*Unique lands possessing basic land types that provide high-impact mechanical utility in specific Commander strategies.*

#### 1. The Throne of Eldraine Monocolored Utility Cycle
*Enters the battlefield tapped unless you control three or more other basic lands of that specific type. Highly fetchable by monocolored and dual fetch lands.*

* [**Mystic Sanctuary**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mystic+Sanctuary") — Land — Island: When it enters untapped, put target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard on top of your library. *Premier fetch target for blue combo/spellslinger.*
* [**Witch's Cottage**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Witch's+Cottage") — Land — Swamp: When it enters untapped, put target creature card from your graveyard on top of your library.
* [**Dwarven Mine**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dwarven+Mine") — Land — Mountain: When it enters untapped, create a 1/1 red Dwarf creature token. *Enables instant Polymorph lines off an end-step fetch.*
* [**Gingerbread Cabin**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Gingerbread+Cabin") — Land — Forest: When it enters untapped, create a Food token.
* [**Idyllic Grange**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Idyllic+Grange") — Land — Plains: When it enters untapped, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature you control.

#### 2. Unique Multicolored & Graveyard Utility
* [**Murmuring Bosk**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Murmuring+Bosk") — Land — Forest:
  * Has the **Forest** basic type (fetchable by all 4 green fetchlands, *Nature's Lore*, *Three Visits*, *Skyshroud Claim*).
  * Taps for {G} safely, or {W}/{B} for 1 life (effectively an Abzan tri-land on a fetchable Forest).
  * Enters tapped unless you reveal a Treefolk card from your hand.
* [**Mistveil Plains**](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mistveil+Plains") — Land — Plains:
  * Has the **Plains** basic type.
  * Enters tapped; {W}, {T}: Put target card from your graveyard on the bottom of its owner's library (activate only if you control two or more white permanents). *Premier engine with Sunforger to recycle instant spells.*
