## 1. Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

* **Final Placement:** **Bracket 3**
* **DTI Threat Index:** `40 / 96` (Score Floor: Bracket 3)
* **Clock Speed (Velocity Vector):** `18 / 48`
* **Opponent Stifle (Suppression Vector):** `13 / 40`
* **WotC Statutory Compliance:** 0 / 3 Game Changers (Floor: Bracket 2)

### Tactical Overview
Hearthhull, the Worldseed commands a synergistic Jund lands-matter deck centered on sacrificing lands for velocity, recurring them from the graveyard, and draining opponents out while amassing huge token armies. Early turns focus on accelerating mana through land-fetch spells and developing board presence. By Turn 4, Hearthhull enters and stations to 2+ charge counters, establishing an engine that draws cards and grants extra land drops each turn. In the mid-game, the deck deploys heavy landfall and sacrifice payoffs such as Titania, Protector of Argoth, Baloth Prime, Omnath, Locus of Rage, and The Gitrog Monster. Once Hearthhull reaches 8+ station counters, it transforms into an evasive 6/7 attacker that drains each opponent for 2 life per sacrificed land. Mass recursion spells like Splendid Reclamation and Aftermath Analyst fuel devastating alpha strikes and burst life loss by Turn 6 to 7. The deck boasts robust recovery and redundancy, though it remains vulnerable to graveyard hate and creature sweepers.

## 2. Operational Primer & Strategy

### Core Strategy
1. Early Ramp & Setup: Spend Turns 1–3 playing ramp spells like Farseek and Nature's Lore and deploying utility creatures to prepare for commander stationing.
2. Deploy the Engine: Cast Hearthhull, the Worldseed on Turn 4, station it to 2+ counters with a utility creature, and activate its ability to draw cards and accelerate land drops.
3. Amass Token Payoffs: Cast premier landfall and sacrifice engines such as Titania, Protector of Argoth, Baloth Prime, and The Gitrog Monster to generate board presence.
4. Mass Recursion & Finish: Station Hearthhull to 8+ counters, cast Splendid Reclamation or activate Aftermath Analyst, and sacrifice lands to drain the table and swing for lethal damage.

### Mulligan Priorities
* **Keep:** ["Hands with 3–4 lands, at least two colors including Green, an early ramp spell (Nature's Lore, Farseek, Sol Ring), and a 2-to-3 mana creature to station Hearthhull on Turn 4."]
* **Avoid:** ['Hands with only tapped utility lands, lack of green mana, or expensive 5+ mana payoffs without early ramp or card draw.']

### Tactical Tips
- Always time your land sacrifices when payoff engines like Titania, Protector of Argoth, Mayhem Devil, or an 8+ counter Hearthhull are active to maximize value and direct damage.
- Use fetchlands and self-sacrificing lands to fill the graveyard rapidly, setting up game-winning turns with Splendid Reclamation and Aftermath Analyst.
- Stationing is a sorcery-speed action: ensure you station Hearthhull before combat so its 8+ counter haste and combat abilities are active when attacking.

## 3. Strategic Weaknesses & Exploits

### Critical Vulnerabilities
- 🔴 Mass Graveyard Hate: Cards like Rest in Peace and Bojuka Bog exile land reserves, neutering mass recursion cards like Splendid Reclamation and Aftermath Analyst.
- 🔴 Vulnerability to Board Wipes: The deck relies heavily on creature-based token armies and station enablers, making sweepers like Farewell and Toxic Deluge disruptive to lethal timing.

### Moderate Friction Points
- 🟡 Lack of Stack Interaction: Operating in Jund colors without blue countermagic leaves the deck unable to interact directly with opposing instant-speed win conditions on the stack.
- 🟡 Sorcery-Speed Stationing: Station counters can only be added at sorcery speed, preventing surprise blocking transformations or reactive drain setups on opponents' turns.

### Minor Exploits
- ⚪ Self-Inflicted Life Loss: Aggressive land sacrifices and pain lands (Llanowar Wastes, Sulfurous Springs) can lower your life total against aggressive burn strategies.
