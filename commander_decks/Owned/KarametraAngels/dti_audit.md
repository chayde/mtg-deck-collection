## 1. Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

* **Final Placement:** **Bracket 4**
* **DTI Threat Index:** `32 / 96` (Score Floor: Bracket 3)
* **Clock Speed (Velocity Vector):** `18 / 48`
* **Opponent Stifle (Suppression Vector):** `13 / 40`
* **WotC Statutory Compliance:** 4 / 3 Game Changers (Floor: Bracket 4)
  * *Game Changers detected:* Aura Shards, Smothering Tithe, Teferi's Protection, Worldly Tutor

### Tactical Overview
This deck is a Selesnya Midrange strategy combining Angel tribal synergies with Karametra's explosive land-ramp engine. In the early game, cheap ramp spells accelerate Karametra onto the battlefield by Turn 4. Once Karametra is established, every subsequent creature cast fetches a Forest or Plains directly onto the battlefield, creating rapid mana acceleration and thinning the library. In the mid-to-late game, this abundant mana powers out high-impact threats such as Avenger of Zendikar, Righteous Valkyrie, Lyra Dawnbringer, and Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite. The deck closes games through overwhelming aerial combat, often boosted by Akroma's Will or Landfall counters. Robust protection pieces like Heroic Intervention, Shalai, Voice of Plenty, and Sigarda, Font of Blessings shield the board against opposing interaction.

## 2. Operational Primer & Strategy

### Core Strategy
1. Accelerate early with 1-2 CMC ramp spells like Nature's Lore and Birds of Paradise to cast Karametra, God of Harvests by Turn 4.
2. Trigger Karametra repeatedly by casting mid-cost creatures and utility Angels, pulling lands from the library to fuel big-mana plays.
3. Deploy board-dominating threats like Avenger of Zendikar, Righteous Valkyrie, or Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite around Turn 6.
4. Overwhelm the table with large flying creatures and finish opponents with an alpha strike backed by Akroma's Will or wide Landfall buffs.

### Mulligan Priorities
* **Keep:** ['Hands with 3-4 lands and at least one 1-2 CMC ramp spell to guarantee a Turn 4 Karametra, ideally with a cheap creature or draw engine to follow up.']
* **Avoid:** ['Hands heavy with 5+ CMC Angels and no early ramp, or hands lacking basic land access to start the engine.']

### Tactical Tips
- Keep Karametra's devotion below seven early if you expect creature exile removal; an indestructible enchantment is much harder for opponents to interact with.
- Hold Heroic Intervention or Akroma's Will open once your aerial engine is assembled to protect your board from sweepers.
- Prioritize fetching Plains cards when Emeria Shepherd is in hand or on the battlefield to trigger direct-to-battlefield reanimation.

## 3. Strategic Weaknesses & Exploits

### Critical Vulnerabilities
- 🔴 Mass exile effects such as Farewell bypass Karametra's indestructible keyword and remove the deck's board and graveyard in one blow.

### Moderate Friction Points
- 🟡 Lack of stack-based countermagic leaves the deck vulnerable to fast noncreature combo decks and early game-ending spells.
- 🟡 Reliance on natural draws for noncreature draw engines can cause the deck to run out of gas if early creatures are removed.

### Minor Exploits
- ⚪ Nonbasic land destruction or search-stax effects like Aven Mindcensor can slow down Karametra's land-fetching triggers.
