Tactical Overview

This deck is a synergistic Rakdos Goblin aristocrats build led by The Great Goblin. The strategy focuses on flooding the battlefield with cheap Goblin tokens and converting creature deaths into card flow through the commander's impulse ability. It burns opponents whenever counters are placed on Goblins, Orcs, or Armies, rewarding mechanics like amass, mentor, and blight. The deck leverages sacrifice outlets like Skirk Prospector and Goblin Bombardment alongside drain payoffs like Pashalik Mons, Bastion of Remembrance, and Boggart Cursecrafter. It can win through explosive combat steps with Shared Animosity or through infinite persist loops using Putrid Goblin, First Day of Class, and any free sacrifice outlet. While vulnerable to heavy enchantment-based stax and exile sweepers, the deck recovers quickly by chaining impulse draws off dying creatures.

Operational Primer & Strategy

Core Turn Strategy

  1. Deploy cheap Goblin token producers and mana ramp during Turns 1 and 2.
  2. Cast The Great Goblin on Turn 3 to prepare your card advantage and burn engine.
  3. Drop sacrifice outlets and counter-placing cards on Turns 4 and 5 to exile cards and drain life totals.
  4. Close the game on Turns 6 and 7 using Shared Animosity combat attacks, mass aristocrat burn, or a Putrid Goblin persist loop.

Mulligan Priorities

Keep: ['Hands with two to three lands, at least one early mana ramp piece, and low-cost Goblin token generators like Dragon Fodder or Mogg War Marshal.']

Avoid: ['Hands without early red and black mana, or hands overloaded with expensive payoffs like Siege-Gang Commander and Bolg of the North without early curve plays.']

Tactical Tips

  • Use your sacrifice outlets in response to opposing removal to trigger The Great Goblin and bank exiled cards for your next turn.
  • Pair Putrid Goblin with First Day of Class and a sacrifice outlet for an infinite damage and infinite mana loop.
  • Remember that The Great Goblin triggers on any counter placed on a Goblin, including -1/-1 counters from blight and quest counters on creatures.

Strategic Weaknesses & Interaction Exploits

Critical Vulnerabilities

  • Complete inability to remove resolved problem enchantments like Rest in Peace, Propaganda, or Humility.
  • Exile-based board sweepers such as Farewell and Sunfall that bypass both regeneration and on-death triggers.

Moderate Friction Points

  • Lack of stack interaction to disrupt opposing fast win attempts or protect key combo turns.
  • Reliance on the graveyard and death triggers makes replacement exile effects shut down primary card flow.

Minor Weaknesses

  • Heavy colored mana requirements on cards like Goblin Chainwhirler can strain awkward mana draws.
  • Vulnerability to repeatable creature token sweepers.
