# Deck Guide: The Great Goblin ("The Goblin King's Court")

### Commander: [The Great Goblin](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/158/the-great-goblin?utm_source=api) ({1}{B/R}{B/R})

> **Origin:** Acquired as a pre-built Commander deck from a local card shop, reconciled against missing inventory, and reinforced with key sacrifice engines and token makers from store inventory.

## Commander Strategy
**The Great Goblin**
*   **Archetype:** Rakdos ({B}{R}) Goblins / Counter Burn & Aristocrats Impulse Velocity
*   **Bracket:** 3 (Upgraded Casual — 0 Game Changers)
*   **DTI Threat Index:** **41 / 96** (Bracket 3 | Velocity: **19/48** | Suppression: **13/40** | Gates: All Passed | [DTI Report](dti_report.html))
*   **Core Goal:** Exploit The Great Goblin's dual engine: whenever you put one or more counters on a Goblin, Orc, or Army you control, deal 2 damage to target opponent; and whenever another Goblin, Orc, or Army you control dies, exile the top card of your library to play until the end of your next turn. Combine free sacrifice outlets, counter placement (+1/+1, -1/-1, quest counters), and recursive loops to burn out the table while maintaining relentless card velocity.

### Three Interlocking Engines & Win Conditions
1.  **The Counter Burn Machine:** Every counter placed on a Goblin, Orc, or Army triggers 2 damage to face. Mentoring with **Goblin Banneret**, adding counters with **Mauhúr, Uruk-hai Captain** and **Uglúk of the White Hand**, entering via **First Day of Class**, or returning via Persist with **Putrid Goblin** repeatedly triggers devastating bursts of direct burn.
2.  **The Aristocrats Impulse Engine:** Free sacrifice outlets (**Skirk Prospector**, **Goblin Turncoat**, **Goblin Bombardment**, **Woe Strider**) let you sacrifice disposable Goblin tokens at instant speed to exile cards off your library that remain playable through the end of your *next* turn, giving you access to huge swaths of your deck on demand.
3.  **The Infinite Persist Combo:** With **Putrid Goblin**, **First Day of Class**, and any free sacrifice outlet (**Goblin Turncoat**, **Skirk Prospector**, **Goblin Bombardment**, or **Woe Strider**), Putrid Goblin returns indefinitely with canceling +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters, dealing **infinite 2-damage pings to the table for an immediate game win**.
