## 1. Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

* **Final Placement:** **Bracket 3**
* **DTI Threat Index:** `32 / 96` (Score Floor: Bracket 3)
* **Clock Speed (Velocity Vector):** `18 / 48`
* **Opponent Stifle (Suppression Vector):** `14 / 40`

  * *Game Changers detected:* Cyclonic Rift

### Tactical Overview
This deck utilizes Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk as a low-cost card-drawing commander that transforms into a massive 8/8 trampling threat. The game plan focuses on aggressive green-based mana acceleration to reach the required six colored mana ({2}{R}{R}{G}{G}) by Turn 5. Once transformed, Hulk synergizes with +1/+1 counter amplifiers like Branching Evolution and Hardened Scales to deal lethal combat damage. A two-card interaction with Caltrops triggers Hulk's Enrage ability on attack, generating infinite untaps, power growth, and extra combat phases to end the game immediately. In standard games without Caltrops, the deck applies overwhelming combat pressure through standalone beaters like Kalonian Hydra and asymmetric finishers like Chandra's Ignition, closing out the game across two opponent response cycles by Turn 7.

## 2. Operational Primer & Strategy

### Core Strategy
1. Deploy Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk on Turn 1 and accelerate mana using 1-2 CMC ramp spells and creatures.
2. Develop the board with counter doublers or draw engines like Garruk's Uprising while hitting land drops.
3. Pay {2}{R}{R}{G}{G} on Turn 4 or 5 to transform Bruce Banner into The Incredible Hulk and attack immediately.
4. Execute the infinite combat combo with Caltrops or eliminate all opponents by Turn 7 using scaling trample damage, fight spells, and Chandra's Ignition.

### Mulligan Priorities
* **Keep:** ['Hands containing 3 lands, access to green and blue mana, and at least one 1-2 CMC ramp piece (Birds of Paradise, Farseek, Three Visits, or Sol Ring) to ensure a Turn 4-5 transformation.']
* **Avoid:** ['Hands without green mana, hands flooded with 5+ CMC creatures without ramp, or hands that rely purely on reactive removal without early development.']

### Tactical Tips
- Transforming Bruce Banner is an activated ability costing {2}{R}{R}{G}{G}; it does not cause summoning sickness if Bruce was cast on an earlier turn.
- Commander tax applies only to casting Bruce Banner from the command zone; the {2}{R}{R}{G}{G} transformation cost on the battlefield never increases.
- When attacking with Caltrops on the battlefield, Caltrops deals 1 damage to Hulk to trigger Enrage, increasing his toughness faster than damage accumulates and granting infinite combats.

## 3. Strategic Weaknesses & Exploits

### Critical Vulnerabilities
- 🔴 Heavy reliance on combat damage leaves the primary plan vulnerable to instant-speed spot exile and bounce before damage resolves.
- 🔴 Absence of stack interaction and counterspells makes the deck susceptible to opposing fast combo wins and non-damage board wipes.

### Moderate Friction Points
- 🟡 The transformation cost of {2}{R}{R}{G}{G} requires specific heavy colored mana fixing ({R}{R}{G}{G}).
- 🟡 Lack of nonland tutors means the deck cannot reliably search for Caltrops or specific silver bullets, depending instead on raw card draw.

### Minor Exploits
- ⚪ Bruce Banner's draw ability is restricted to sorcery speed, limiting reactive mana usage during opponents' turns.
- ⚪ Wide token strategies can absorb single-combat attacks if trample enablers or board wipes are not drawn.
