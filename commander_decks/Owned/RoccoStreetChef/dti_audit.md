## 1. Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

* **Final Placement:** **Bracket 3**
* **DTI Threat Index:** `46 / 96` (Score Floor: Bracket 3)
* **Clock Speed (Velocity Vector):** `24 / 48`
* **Opponent Stifle (Suppression Vector):** `16 / 40`
* **WotC Statutory Compliance:** 0 / 3 Game Changers (Floor: Bracket 2)

### Tactical Overview
This Rocco, Street Chef deck is an aggressive Naya Food and exile-matters midrange strategy. It establishes its primary engine early by casting Rocco on Turn 2 or 3, generating continuous card advantage and token creation whenever cards are played from exile. The deck converts Food tokens and +1/+1 counters into explosive mana and card flow using key enablers like Jaheira, Friend of the Forest, Academy Manufactor, and Baylen, the Haymaker. Without relying on infinite combos, it closes games through overwhelming combat damage and burn, utilizing overrun effects like Night of the Sweets' Revenge alongside direct damage engines such as Shalai and Hallar and All Will Be One. The deck features robust removal and high card velocity, though it remains vulnerable to mass exile wipes and provides symmetrical card access to opponents.

---

## 2. Operational Primer & Strategy

### Core Strategy
1. Accelerate mana on Turns 1–2 using one-drop dorks or two-drop ramp spells to cast Rocco, Street Chef ahead of curve.
2. Deploy Rocco on Turn 2 or 3 to begin exiling cards on end steps, triggering +1/+1 counters and Food creation on subsequent turns.
3. Leverage token converters like Academy Manufactor, Jaheira, Friend of the Forest, and Peregrin Took to turn Food into massive mana and card velocity.
4. Close the game on Turns 5–7 using massive overrun attacks via Night of the Sweets' Revenge, giant unblockable threats like Feasting Hobbit, or direct burn from All Will Be One and Shalai and Hallar.

### Mulligan Priorities
* **Keep:** ['Hands with 2–3 lands, at least one 1- or 2-mana ramp piece to cast Rocco, Street Chef on Turn 2 or 3, and a token payoff or interaction spell.']
* **Avoid:** ['Hands lacking green mana, hands with only expensive 4+ mana payoffs and no early acceleration, or hands without colored fixing for Naya.']

### Tactical Tips
- Prioritize casting spells from exile over hand spells to trigger Rocco, Street Chef and grow your creatures with +1/+1 counters.
- Save Delayed Blast Fireball to cast from exile via Rocco or foretell, turning it into a one-sided 5-damage board wipe.
- Use Inspiring Call or Plaza of Heroes reactively to protect your key engine pieces against opposing destroy wipes and spot removal.

## 3. Strategic Weaknesses & Exploits

### Critical Vulnerabilities
- 🔴 Heavy reliance on creature and artifact permanents makes the board vulnerable to comprehensive sweepers like Farewell or Vandalblast.
- 🔴 Lack of stack interaction or counterspells leaves the deck exposed to fast opposing combo wins.

### Moderate Friction Points
- 🟡 Symmetrical card access from Rocco, Street Chef can hand opponents answers or acceleration if they utilize exile cards effectively.
- 🟡 Absence of creature tutors requires relying on draw velocity to find specific finishers like All Will Be One.

### Minor Exploits
- ⚪ Targeted removal on Rocco before the end step delays initial token production and counter distribution.
