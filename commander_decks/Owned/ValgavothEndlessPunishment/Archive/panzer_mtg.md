## 📖 Executive Summary & Core Strategy

Panzer MTG took a radical, hyper-aggressive approach to upgrading *Endless Punishment*, swapping out **64 cards** from the preconstructed deck. His philosophy cuts the deck's average nonland mana value down to ~2.5 CMC, adds fast mana, removes almost all high-CMC demons, and layers in **two-card infinite win conditions**.

### Key Strategic Pillars
1. **Aggressive Action Taxes:** Floods the board with low-CMC punishers ([Roiling Vortex](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Roiling+Vortex"), [Manabarbs](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Manabarbs"), [Spellshock](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Spellshock"), [Zo-Zu the Punisher](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zo-Zu+the+Punisher"), [Ankh of Mishra](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ankh+of+Mishra"), [Razorkin Needlehead](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Razorkin+Needlehead")).
2. **Infinite Combos:** 
   * [Exquisite Blood](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Exquisite+Blood") + [Sanguine Bond](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sanguine+Bond") / [Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vito,+Thorn+of+the+Dusk+Rose") / [Marauding Blight-Priest](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Marauding+Blight-Priest") $\rightarrow$ Infinite drain loop.
   * [Bloodchief Ascension](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bloodchief+Ascension") + [Mindcrank](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mindcrank") $\rightarrow$ Infinite mill and life drain.
3. **Low Curve & Fetchlands:** Runs 7 fetchlands to thin the deck and ensure untapped mana.

### Critical Flaws & Why We Deviated
1. **The $75 Nonbo:** Panzer ran [Sulfuric Vortex](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sulfuric+Vortex") alongside [Exquisite Blood](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Exquisite+Blood"). Because *Sulfuric Vortex* states *"Players can't gain life"*, it completely shuts off *Exquisite Blood*, turning his own $35 combo piece into a dead card and disabling his lifegain cushion.
2. **Severely Mana-Starved:** Running only 33 physical lands + MDFCs in a 4-CMC commander deck that draws massive table removal causes the deck to stall if fast mana rocks are countered or removed.
3. **Cut Iconic Demon Anchors:** Panzer cut [Mogis, God of Slaughter](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mogis,+God+of+Slaughter") and [The Lord of Pain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"The+Lord+of+Pain"), which we specifically protected per user preference.
