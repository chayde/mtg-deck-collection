## 📖 Executive Summary & Core Strategy

Joey Schultz's *Upping the Average* tackles the single biggest tactical dilemma of playing Valgavoth: **how to keep him alive when the entire table views you as Public Enemy #1**. Group slug decks naturally create table hostility, and Valgavoth's *Ward (Pay 2 life)* does not stop opponents from targeting him. Joey's build focuses heavily on cheap commander protection, attack deterrence, and goad.

### Key Strategic Pillars
1. **"Overboard" Commander Protection:** Because Valgavoth is the deck's primary card draw engine, Joey prioritizes keeping him alive with [Champion's Helm](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Champion's+Helm") (Equip 1 Hexproof), [Untimely Malfunction](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Untimely+Malfunction") (Modal redirect), [Bolt Bend](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bolt+Bend"), and [Kaya's Ghostform](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Kaya's+Ghostform") (1-mana destroy and exile insurance).
2. **Combat Deterrence & Goad:** Uses [Revenge of Ravens](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Revenge+of+Ravens") to punish attackers and trigger Valgavoth on enemy attack steps, alongside goad cards like [Disrupt Decorum](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Disrupt+Decorum"), [Vengeful Ancestor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vengeful+Ancestor"), and [Kardur, Doomscourge](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Kardur,+Doomscourge") to force opponents to attack each other.
3. **Rigorous Precon Cuts:** Cut clunky precon holdovers like [Decree of Pain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Decree+of+Pain"), [Sadistic Shell Game](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sadistic+Shell+Game"), [Séance Board](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"S%C3%A9ance+Board"), [Light Up the Stage](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Light+Up+the+Stage"), [Theater of Horrors](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Theater+of+Horrors"), and [Stormfist Crusader](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Stormfist+Crusader").

### Constraints & Where Our Build Differs
1. **Strict Budget Ceiling:** Due to the series rules, Joey could not include premium staples like [Sheoldred, the Apocalypse](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sheoldred,+the+Apocalypse") ($70), [Orcish Bowmasters](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Orcish+Bowmasters") ($35), [Bloodthirsty Conqueror](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bloodthirsty+Conqueror") ($35), [Deflecting Swat](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Deflecting+Swat") ($70), or [Deadly Rollick](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Deadly+Rollick") ($28).
2. **Retained Filter Lands & Rocks:** Joey kept [Graven Cairns](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Graven+Cairns"), [Shadowblood Ridge](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Shadowblood+Ridge"), and [Rakdos Signet](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Rakdos+Signet") due to budget constraints, whereas our build purged all filter cards.
