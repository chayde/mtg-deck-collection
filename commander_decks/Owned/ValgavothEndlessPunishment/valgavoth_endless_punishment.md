## Commander Strategy
**Valgavoth, Harrower of Souls**
*   **Archetype:** Rakdos ({B}{R}) Group Slug, Triggered Life Loss & Card Advantage Engine
*   **Bracket:** **2 (Core / Precon Baseline — 0 Game Changers)**
*   **Target Upgrade Bracket:** Bracket 3 (Upgraded Casual)
*   **DTI Threat Index:** **28 / 96** (Bracket 2 | Velocity: **14/48** | Suppression: **10/40** | Clock: Onset Turn 7, Ready Turn 6 | Gates: All 4 Passed)
*   **Core Goal:** Deploy passive punisher permanents that cause opponents to lose life on their own turns (tapping mana, casting spells, drawing cards, or taking combat damage). Valgavoth converts the first instance of life loss per opponent's turn into a fresh card drawn and a +1/+1 counter, transforming symmetrical group slug into asymmetrical card advantage and a rapidly scaling flying threat protected by Ward—Pay 2 life.

### 🎯 Tactical Overview & Engine Dynamics
*   **The Valgavoth Engine:** Traditional Rakdos group slug commanders (like Mogis) suffer from severe card starvation because they deal damage without advancing your card flow. Valgavoth solves this completely: every opponent turn where an opponent loses at least 1 life yields **+1 card** and **+1/+1 counter** (drawing up to 3 extra cards per turn cycle).
*   **Turn Cycle Priority:** 
    1. *Turns 1–3:* Lay down ramp rocks and low-CMC passive life-loss pieces (Blood Seeker, Harsh Mentor, Stormfist Crusader).
    2. *Turn 4:* Cast Valgavoth with open board triggers or instant-speed pings ready to trigger on opponents' turns.
    3. *Turns 5+:* Accumulate card velocity, deploy board control, and swing with an evasive 7/7+ demon while life totals crumble.
