# Deck Guide: Smaug the Impenetrable ("The Golden Hoard & Dragonfire Masochism")

### Commander: [Smaug the Impenetrable](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/9/smaug-the-impenetrable?utm_source=api) ({5}{B}{R})

> **Origin:** Acquired as a pre-built Commander deck from a local game store (LGS), reconciled against store inventory, reinforced with a 38th land (basic Swamp) to achieve a legal 100-card singleton baseline, and synchronized to MTG Forge.

## Commander Strategy
**Smaug the Impenetrable**
*   **Archetype:** Rakdos ({B}{R}) Self-Damage Treasures / Aristocrat Burn & Dragon Masochism
*   **Bracket:** 3 (Upgraded Casual — 1 Game Changer: *Orcish Bowmasters*)
*   **DTI Threat Index:** **39 / 96** (Solid Bracket 3 | Velocity: **22/48** | Suppression: **15/40** | Clock: Onset Turn 5, Ready Turn 5, 1 Response Cycle | Gates: All Passed | [DTI Report](dti_report.html))
*   **Core Goal:** Exploit Smaug's unique combination of 8/7 stats, flying, haste, indestructible, and masochistic trigger: *"Whenever Smaug is dealt noncombat damage, create that many Treasure tokens."* Turn symmetrical sweepers (**Blasphemous Act**, **Star of Extinction**, **Chain Reaction**), cheap burn (**Flame Slash**, **Cut Propulsion**), and damage-reflecting hardware (**Blazing Sunsteel**, **Pariah's Shield**) into volcanic bursts of 13–20+ Treasure tokens without harming Smaug, then convert that hoard into instant-kill drain triggers (**Mirkwood Bats**, **Marionette Master**, **Mayhem Devil**) or massive lethal finishers (**Exsanguinate**, **Jaya's Immolating Inferno**).

### Official DeckCheck Operational Primer
*   **Turn 1–3 (Acceleration):** Accelerate mana using 2-CMC rocks (**Arcane Signet**, **Talisman of Indulgence**, **Fellwar Stone**), rituals (**Dark Ritual**, **Geosurge**), and discard-to-draw treasure spells (**Deadly Dispute**, **Faithless Looting**).
*   **Turn 4–5 (Deployment):** Deploy Smaug the Impenetrable on Turn 4 or 5 and retain mana or treasures for instant/sorcery noncombat damage spells in the same window.
*   **Turn 5 (Mass Conversion):** Cast high-yield damage spells like **Cut Propulsion** (16 damage), **Blasphemous Act** (13 damage), or **Star of Extinction** (20 damage) targeting Smaug to produce a massive wave of 13 to 20 Treasure tokens.
*   **Turn 5–6 (Game Conversion):** Funnel generated Treasures into aristocrat drain triggers from **Mirkwood Bats** and **Marionette Master**, loop damage reflection via **Blazing Sunsteel**, or cast a game-ending **Exsanguinate** or **Jaya's Immolating Inferno**.

### Mulligan Priorities & Tactical Tips
*   **Keep:** Hands with 2–3 lands, at least two early ramp pieces or rituals (**Sol Ring**, **Arcane Signet**, **Talisman of Indulgence**, **Deadly Dispute**), and at least one noncombat damage trigger or payoff spell.
*   **Avoid:** Hands that lack early ramp, hands saturated with expensive 5+ CMC payoffs without acceleration, or hands with no colored mana fixing.
*   **Protection Priority:** Leverage **Bolt Bend**, **Ricochet Trap**, and **Return the Favor** to redirect single-target exile spells like *Swords to Plowshares* or *Chaos Warp* away from Smaug.
*   **Asymmetric Wipes:** Remember that **Blasphemous Act** and **Star of Extinction** wipe opposing creatures while fueling your Treasure generation simultaneously.
*   **Card Flow:** Use **Professional Face-Breaker** or **Deadly Dispute** to dig deep into your library once Smaug produces 10+ Treasures.

### Strategic Weaknesses & Failure Modes
*   **Critical:** Single-target exile and bounce effects (*Swords to Plowshares*, *Cyclonic Rift*, *Farewell*) that bypass Smaug's indestructible keyword; heavy commander tax if Smaug is countered or removed before generating his first batch of Treasures.
*   **Moderate:** Artifact and token hate pieces like *Collector Ouphe*, *Null Rod*, or *Karn, the Great Creator* that shut down Treasure activations; narrow enchantment removal in Rakdos.
*   **Minor:** Graveyard hate against incidental recursion (**Rivaz of the Claw**, **Molten Gatekeeper**); early aggressive combat pressure while spending turns deploying ramp and high-cost spells.

### Three Interlocking Engines & Win Conditions
1.  **The Masochism Treasure Geyser:** Because Smaug is indestructible, damage does not destroy him. Casting **Blasphemous Act** ({8}{R}) for 1 mana wipes the opponents' boards while dealing 13 damage to Smaug, creating 13 Treasure tokens on the spot. Casting **Star of Extinction** ({5}{R}{R}) creates 20 Treasures. Resolving **Cut Propulsion** ({2}{R}) forces Smaug (8 power, flying) to deal 16 damage to himself, netting 16 Treasures for just 3 mana.
2.  **The Artifact Aristocrats & Token Burn Engine:** As massive piles of Treasures enter and are cracked for mana, payoff pieces drain the table relentlessly. **Mirkwood Bats** drains 1 life on creation AND 1 life on sacrifice (26 life drained from a 13-Treasure burst). **Marionette Master** drains 4 life per Treasure sacrificed. **Disciple of the Vault**, **Marionette Apprentice**, **Mayhem Devil**, **Nadier's Nightblade**, and **Reckless Fireweaver** turn any burst of mana into immediate lethal table burn.
3.  **The Blazing Sunsteel Engine:** Equipping Smaug with **Blazing Sunsteel** ({1}{R}) establishes a devastating damage-reflection loop: whenever Smaug is dealt damage, Sunsteel triggers to deal that much damage to any target. When targeted at Smaug, Smaug's indestructible body absorbs the blow, triggers to create that many Treasures, and re-triggers Sunsteel to repeat the loop indefinitely for infinite Treasures and infinite direct burn to opponents' faces.

## 🛠️ Card Explanations

### 🔥 Self-Damage & Masochism Triggers (10 Cards)
*   **[Blasphemous Act](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Blasphemous+Act"):** Premier mass sweeper dealing 13 damage to all creatures; triggers Smaug to produce 13 Treasures for as little as {R}.
*   **[Star of Extinction](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Star+of+Extinction"):** Nuclear board wipe dealing 20 damage to all creatures and planeswalkers, generating 20 Treasures off Smaug while destroying a problem land.
*   **[Chain Reaction](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Chain+Reaction"):** Scalable board wipe dealing X damage to each creature; turns opposing board states into X Treasures.
*   **[Cut Propulsion](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cut+Propulsion"):** Forces target creature to deal self-damage equal to power (doubled if flying); on Smaug, deals 16 damage to create 16 Treasures instantly.
*   **[Pyrohemia](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Pyrohemia"):** Repeatable {R} activation dealing 1 damage to each creature and player; machine-guns the board and converts red mana into Treasures.
*   **[Flame Slash](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Flame+Slash"):** 1-mana burn dealing 4 damage to Smaug to net 4 Treasures.
*   **[Chain Lightning](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Chain+Lightning"):** 1-mana burn dealing 3 damage to Smaug to produce 3 Treasures.
*   **[Ghostfire Slice](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ghostfire+Slice"):** Deploys 4 damage to Smaug (often discounted if opponents control commanders) to forge 4 Treasures.
*   **[Fire Covenant](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Fire+Covenant"):** Instant-speed asymmetrical board clear and mana conversion; converts life total into direct damage to opponents' boards and Smaug for Treasures.
*   **[The Last Agni Kai](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"The+Last+Agni+Kai"):** Forces Smaug to fight an opposing creature, creating noncombat damage return triggers, banking excess damage as {R}, and preserving unspent red mana across phases.

### 🛡️ Treasure Amplifiers & Masochism Hardware (4 Cards)
*   **[Blazing Sunsteel](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Blazing+Sunsteel"):** Equipment that reflects damage dealt to equipped creature to any target; creates an explosive damage loop on Smaug to generate massive Treasures and direct burn.
*   **[Pariah's Shield](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Pariah's+Shield"):** Redirects all damage dealt to you directly onto Smaug; renders you invulnerable while turning incoming enemy attacks into massive Treasure hordes.
*   **[Pain for All](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Pain+for+All"):** Aura that deals Smaug's 8 power to any target on ETB, and reflects any damage dealt to Smaug directly to each opponent.
*   **[Dictate of the Twin Gods](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dictate+of+the+Twin+Gods"):** Flash damage doubler that doubles damage dealt to Smaug (doubling Treasure production) and doubles damage dealt to opponents.

### 💰 Treasure & Artifact Aristocrats / Burn Payoffs (12 Cards)
*   **[Marionette Master](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Marionette+Master"):** Fabricate 3 threat that drains target opponent for 4 life whenever an artifact/Treasure is sacrificed.
*   **[Marionette Apprentice](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Marionette+Apprentice"):** Drains each opponent for 1 life whenever an artifact or creature dies.
*   **[Mirkwood Bats](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mirkwood+Bats"):** Devastating drain engine that pings each opponent for 1 life whenever you create OR sacrifice a token.
*   **[Mayhem Devil](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mayhem+Devil"):** Pings any target for 1 damage whenever any player sacrifices a permanent.
*   **[Disciple of the Vault](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Disciple+of+the+Vault"):** 1-drop aristocrat that drains target opponent for 1 life whenever an artifact is put into a graveyard.
*   **[Nadier's Nightblade](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nadier's+Nightblade"):** Drains each opponent for 1 life and heals you for 1 whenever a token leaves your battlefield.
*   **[Reckless Fireweaver](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Reckless+Fireweaver"):** Pings each opponent for 1 damage whenever an artifact enters under your control.
*   **[Molten Gatekeeper](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Molten+Gatekeeper"):** Pings each opponent on creature entry with unearth recursion.
*   **[Juri, Master of the Revue](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Juri,+Master+of+the+Revue"):** Grows massive with +1/+1 counters as Treasures are sacrificed, threatening a lethal death fling.
*   **[Smaug the Magnificent](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Smaug+the+Magnificent"):** Hasty dragon attacker that deals damage equal to your Treasure count to any target on attack.
*   **[Broadside Bombardiers](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Broadside+Bombardiers"):** Boast cannon that flings artifacts or creatures to deal scalable damage to any target (including Smaug).
*   **[The Sackville-Bagginses](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"The+Sackville-Bagginses"):** Sacrifices tokens to draw and ping opponents for 1 life whenever tokens are sacrificed.

### 🎯 Finishing Sinks & Outlets (2 Cards)
*   **[Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Exsanguinate"):** Devastating X-cost mana sink funded by massive Treasure hordes to drain all opponents simultaneously.
