## 1. Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

* **Final Placement:** **Bracket 3**
* **DTI Threat Index:** `40 / 96` (Score Floor: Bracket 3)
* **Clock Speed (Velocity Vector):** `18 / 48`
* **Opponent Stifle (Suppression Vector):** `12 / 40`
* **WotC Statutory Compliance:** 0 / 3 Game Changers (Floor: Bracket 2)

### Tactical Overview
This Gruul deck is an explosive big-mana stompy strategy built around Etali, Primal Conqueror. The primary game plan focuses on rapid early ramp using 1-mana dorks, land-fetching sorceries, and cost reducers to cast Etali ahead of curve by Turn 4. Upon entering the battlefield, Etali immediately triggers to cast up to four free nonland spells from all players' libraries. The deck magnifies this overwhelming card advantage using a deep suite of temporary clone spells, blink effects, and trigger multipliers like Twinflame, Heat Shimmer, Orthion, Hero of Lavabrink, and Panharmonicon. Each additional trigger generates exponential board presence and card velocity. The deck closes out games either by overwhelming the table with large stolen boards and token copies or by transforming Etali into Etali, Primal Sickness, an 11/11 indestructible trampler that eliminates opponents in a single swing via poison counters. While highly explosive and resilient to countermagic thanks to redundancy, it relies on proactive threats over defensive interaction.

## 2. Operational Primer & Strategy

### Core Strategy
1. Accelerate Mana: Use one-drop mana elves, land-ramp spells, and mana rocks on Turns 1 through 3 to reach seven mana as fast as possible.
2. Deploy the Commander: Cast Etali, Primal Conqueror on Turn 4 to exile and cast four free nonland spells from across the table.
3. Multiply Triggers: Use temporary clone spells like Twinflame and permanents like Panharmonicon to copy Etali repeatedly for massive card and mana advantage.
4. Close the Game: Overwhelm the board with stolen threats or pay nine mana to transform Etali into Etali, Primal Sickness and eliminate players with 11 trample infect damage.

### Mulligan Priorities
* **Keep:** ["Hands with 2 to 3 lands and at least two cheap ramp pieces (such as Llanowar Elves, Wild Growth, or Nature's Lore) that guarantee reaching six or seven mana by Turn 4."]
* **Avoid:** ['Hands with multiple 5+ mana payoffs, clone spells without early ramp, or insufficient colored mana sources.']

### Tactical Tips
- Remember that token copies of legendary creatures will trigger enter-the-battlefield abilities before you must sacrifice one to the legend rule.
- Prioritize casting opponents' card draw, extra turns, or high-impact permanents off Etali triggers before resolving low-impact utility spells.
- Use Temur Sabertooth or Voyager Staff to save Etali from targeted exile or board wipes while resetting its powerful entrance trigger.

## 3. Strategic Weaknesses & Exploits

### Critical Vulnerabilities
- 🔴 Rule of Law effects and heavy stax (such as Drannith Magistrate or Archon of Emeria) completely shut down Etali's ability to cast exiled spells.
- 🔴 Targeted counterspells against the initial seven-mana commander cast cause severe tempo loss and add commander tax.

### Moderate Friction Points
- 🟡 Low density of instant-speed interaction leaves the deck vulnerable to faster combo strategies before Turn 4.
- 🟡 Dependence on opponents' libraries can occasionally result in low-impact hits against creature-light or synergy-dependent decks.

### Minor Exploits
- ⚪ Vulnerability to artifact or creature board wipes after overcommitting cloned boards.
- ⚪ High reliance on basic Forest and Mountain access for early land-ramp spells.
