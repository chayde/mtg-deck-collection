## 📖 Executive Summary & Strategic Comparison

Guys That Magic's two-part upgrade pivots the *Star Trek Commander* precon into a 7-CMC commander **Blink / Clone / Theft** shell centered on re-triggering **The Borg Queen**'s ETB *Assimilate* ability and copying high-value permanents.

### Key Strategic Takeaways
1. **Blink & Panharmonicon Package:** Lean heavily into cheap blink instants ([Cloudshift](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cloudshift"), [Ephemerate](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ephemerate"), [Essence Flux](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Essence+Flux"), [Getaway Glamer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Getaway+Glamer"), [Parting Gust](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Parting+Gust")) alongside permanent doublers/blinkers ([Thassa, Deep-Dwelling](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Thassa,+Deep-Dwelling"), [Displacer Kitten](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Displacer+Kitten"), [Aminatou, the Fateshifter](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Aminatou,+the+Fateshifter"), [Panharmonicon](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Panharmonicon"), [Roaming Throne](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Roaming+Throne"), [The Mind Stone](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"The+Mind+Stone")) and clone/artifact payoffs ([Ultron, Artificial Malevolence](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ultron,+Artificial+Malevolence"), [Vesuvan Duplimancy](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vesuvan+Duplimancy"), [Simulacrum Synthesizer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Simulacrum+Synthesizer")).
2. **Top Phase 2 Takeaways for Our Build:** [Vault of Champions](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vault+of+Champions"), [Roaming Throne](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Roaming+Throne"), and [Simulacrum Synthesizer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Simulacrum+Synthesizer").

### Key Weaknesses vs. Our Phase 1 Swarm/Double-Strike Build
1. **Retained Filter Land & Slow Precon Fillers:** Kept *Skycloud Expanse* (filter land) and 4 slow precon theft/combat fillers (*Assimilation Assessor*, *Assimilate the Wounded*, *Hive Mind Coprocessor*, *Gift of Perfection*).
2. **Cut High-Velocity Staples:** Removed *Skullclamp*, *Thirst for Knowledge*, *Wayfarer's Bauble*, *Everflowing Chalice*, and *Astral Cornucopia*, leaving our Phase 1 Swarm / Double-Strike / *Urza* build more resilient and consistent.
