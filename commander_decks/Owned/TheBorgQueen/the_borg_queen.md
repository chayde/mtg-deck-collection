### Commander: [The Borg Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/trc/1/the-borg-queen?utm_source=api) ({4}{W}{U}{B})

> **Origin & Build Status:** Evolved from the *Star Trek Commander* (`TRC #1`) Preconstructed Deck via an approved **15-card Phase 1 Bracket 3 overhaul** ([Visual Swap Matrix](swap_matrix.html) · 15-card order placed **`$102.62`** across 4 Manapool packages tracked in [`order_tracking.md`](order_tracking.md)). Verified via Scryfall (**0 / 3 Game Changers**), 20-game Bracket 3 Goldfish simulation, DTI threat audit (`38/96`), and 100-game MTG Forge `2.0.16` pod gauntlet (`FORGE_POD_LOG.md`).

## Commander Strategy
**The Borg Queen**
*   **Archetype:** Esper ({W}{U}{B}) Artifact Creature Swarm, Double Strike & Assimilate Midrange
*   **Bracket:** **3 (Upgraded — 0 / 3 Game Changers)**
*   **DTI Threat Index:** **38 / 96** (Bracket 3 | Velocity: **21/48** | Suppression: **15/40** | Clock: Onset Turn 6, Ready Turn 5, 1 Response Cycle | Gates: All 4 Passed | [DTI Audit](dti_audit.md) | [DTI Visual Report](dti_report.html))
*   **Goldfish Telemetry (Phase 1 Upgraded, B3):** **84% Commander Cast Rate (67/80 seats, Avg Turn 6.9, Fastest Turn 3)** | **39% Gold / 59% Silver / 2% Desperation Keeps** (6.89 avg hand size) | **60% Bracket 3 Readiness (T≤7)** ([Goldfish Log](GOLDFISH_LOG.md) | [Goldfish Report](goldfish_report.html))
*   **ForgeSim 4-AI Pod Telemetry (MTG Forge 2.0.16, 100 Games):** **44.0% Win Rate (44/100 vs. Rocco 12%, JinSakai 7%, Gisa 28%)** | **80.0% Commander Cast Rate (Avg Turn 8.0)** | **44.0% Gold / 55.0% Silver / 0.0% Desperation Keeps** | **75.1 Avg Peak Power / 99.5 Avg Combat Damage** ([Forge Pod Log](FORGE_POD_LOG.md) | [Forge Pod Report](forge_pod_report.html))
*   **Core Goal:** Flood the board with 1/1 colorless Borg, Thopter, and Gnome artifact creature tokens using cheap artifact cost reduction (**Etherium Sculptor**), combat-damage token multipliers (**Locutus of Borg**, **Antagonist Incorporator**), and perpetual engines (**Illustrious Wanderglyph**, **Sai, Master Thopterist**, **Urza, Lord High Artificer**). Protect your board at flash speed (**Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter**, **Emergency Regeneration**, **Akroma's Will**), sweep away nonartifact creatures asymmetrically (**Organic Extinction**, **Battle of Wolf 359**, **The Flesh Is Weak**), and convert wide artifact armies into lethal multi-player swings through **Primary Unimatrix Adjunct** (team double strike), **Cyberdrive Awakener** (flying + 4/4 mana rocks), **One, Borg Evolved**, and **The Borg Queen** (+2/+2 + Assimilate).
