Tactical Overview

Phase 1 Bracket 3 upgrade of The Borg Queen ({4}{W}{U}{B}) Esper Artifact Creature Swarm deck (0 / 3 Game Changers). Retaining The Borg Queen's +2/+2 anthem and Assimilate engine alongside Queen's Diamond and Locutus of Borg, this 15-card overhaul cuts Skycloud Expanse, sluggish 4-CMC rocks, and off-plan nonbos to inject untapped Battlebond fixing (Sea of Clouds), 2-CMC velocity (Fellwar Stone, Etherium Sculptor, Antagonist Incorporator), flash speed (Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter), artifact token multipliers (Sai, Master Thopterist, Illustrious Wanderglyph, Urza, Lord High Artificer), asymmetrical Improvise removal (Organic Extinction, Path to Exile, The Flesh Is Weak), and decisive flying/double-strike finishers (Primary Unimatrix Adjunct, Cyberdrive Awakener, Akroma's Will).

Operational Primer & Strategy

Core Turn Strategy

  1. Turns 1–3: Curve out with untapped duals (Sea of Clouds, Morphic Pool, Spire of Industry), 2-CMC rocks (Talismans, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone), Etherium Sculptor, Wayfarer's Bauble, and early Borg/draw pieces (Antagonist Incorporator, Command Subadjunct, Extraction Integrant, Maturation Chamber, Skullclamp).
  2. Turns 4–5: Establish token and mana engines with Urza, Lord High Artificer, Sai, Master Thopterist, Illustrious Wanderglyph, Locutus of Borg, Co-Option Adjutant, Type-03 Borg Vessel, or Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter (allowing end-step artifact deployment).
  3. Turns 5–6: Cast The Borg Queen ({4}{W}{U}{B}) via Urza mana or a charged Queen's Diamond to grant +2/+2 across all artifact creatures and assimilate opponents' top-deck threats on ETB and attack.
  4. Close-Out: Break board stalls cleanly via Organic Extinction or Battle of Wolf 359, then end the game in one combat step with Cyberdrive Awakener, Primary Unimatrix Adjunct, One, Borg Evolved, or Akroma's Will.

Mulligan Priorities

Keep: ['Hands with 3+ lands (preferring untapped sources like Sea of Clouds, Command Tower, or Morphic Pool), at least one 1–2 CMC ramp/discount piece (Sol Ring, Talismans, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Etherium Sculptor), and an early draw or token piece (Skullclamp, Voyage Home, Antagonist Incorporator, Maturation Chamber).']

Avoid: ["All-tapped land openers lacking 2-CMC acceleration, or clunky 6–7 CMC hands without Urza, Queen's Diamond, or draw velocity."]

Tactical Tips

  • Urza, Lord High Artificer turns every summoning-sick Borg token, Thopter, Gnome, and Clue/Spacecraft into {U} on the turn it enters, accelerating 7-CMC The Borg Queen as early as Turn 3–5.
  • With Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter on board, hold up interaction (Akroma's Will, Emergency Regeneration, Disallow, Path to Exile) and flash in Illustrious Wanderglyph or Cyberdrive Awakener on the last opponent's end step.
  • Pair Antagonist Incorporator and Locutus of Borg with Primary Unimatrix Adjunct or Akroma's Will: double strike triggers combat-damage token generation and Co-Option Adjutant card draw twice per creature.
  • The Flesh Is Weak puts a +1/+1 counter on nonartifact creatures like Sai, Master Thopterist and Urza, Lord High Artificer—converting them into artifacts so they benefit from The Borg Queen, Tempered Steel, and survive Organic Extinction and Battle of Wolf 359.

Strategic Weaknesses & Interaction Exploits

Critical Vulnerabilities

  • 7-CMC commander cost paired with 12 remaining precon taplands (cycling duals, Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse, Arcane Sanctum, Path of Ancestry) still yields a Turn 6.9 average deployment (60% T<=7 Bracket 3 readiness) until Phase 2 fetch/shock/bond land upgrades.
  • Broad artifact exile sweepers (Farewell, Austere Command) still hit artifact creatures and noncreature rocks simultaneously unless shielded by Emergency Regeneration or Akroma's Will.

Moderate Friction Points

  • Zero instant/sorcery tutors for noncreature finishers keeps Akroma's Will and Tempered Steel dependent on draw velocity (Voyage Home, Co-Option Adjutant, Skullclamp).
  • Anthem stacking (+2/+2 from The Borg Queen, Tempered Steel, Illustrious Wanderglyph) turns off Skullclamp's effortless 1-toughness token sacrifice outlet late game (use Sai, Master Thopterist or Reclamation Specialist instead).

Minor Weaknesses

  • Reliance on combat phase makes Propaganda/Ghostly Prison effects mildly annoying until answered by Anguished Unmaking or overwhelmed with Urza mana.
