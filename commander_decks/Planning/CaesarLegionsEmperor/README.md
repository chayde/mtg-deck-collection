## Strategic Summary
Operates as a **Mardu Tokens, Aristocrats & Burn Engine**. Caesar functions primarily as a backline tactical commander, triggering whenever any creature attacks. By sacrificing expendable 1/1 tokens (Soldiers, Thopters, Gnomes), Caesar generates card draw velocity, produces replacement hasty attacking tokens, and unleashes direct-damage burn. 

The strategy scales rapidly through token multipliers ([Mondrak, Glory Dominus](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mondrak,+Glory+Dominus"), [Anointed Procession](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Anointed+Procession")), combat doubling ([Isshin, Two Heavens as One](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Isshin,+Two+Heavens+as+One")), aerial ascension ([Divine Visitation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Divine+Visitation")), and anthem burn ([Warleader's Call](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Warleader's+Call"), [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Purphoros,+God+of+the+Forge"), [Impact Tremors](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Impact+Tremors")). Premium protection ([Teferi's Protection](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Teferi's+Protection"), [Flawless Maneuver](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Flawless+Maneuver"), [Boros Charm](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Boros+Charm"), [Grand Crescendo](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Grand+Crescendo")) safeguards the wide board against sweepers.
