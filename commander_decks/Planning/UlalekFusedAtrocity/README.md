# Deck Guide: Ulalek, Fused Atrocity (Eldrazi Stack Incursion)

![Ulalek, Fused Atrocity](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/f/d/fdad1b0e-d3cc-4dd4-ae32-9fce59d047e1.jpg)

## Commander Strategy
**Ulalek, Fused Atrocity** ({C/W}{C/U}{C/B}{C/R}{C/G})
*   **Archetype:** 5-Color Devoid / Eldrazi Tribal & Stack Duplication Engine
*   **Bracket:** 3 (0 Game Changers — 100% Pure Synergy & Velocity)
*   **Core Goal:** Ramp into huge colorless and devoid Eldrazi spells, then use Ulalek's {C}{C} trigger to copy all spells and abilities on the stack, creating exponential board states, double on-cast triggers, and overwhelming annihilator combat steps.

### The Stack Multiplier Engine
1. **On-Cast Duplication:** Ulalek triggers when you *cast* an Eldrazi spell. Paying {C}{C} copies the creature spell itself *and* all other spells and abilities currently on the stack.
2. **Double Cast Triggers:** When you cast titans like *Ulamog, the Defiler* (exiling half a library) or *Artisan of Kozilek* (reanimating a creature), paying {C}{C} copies both the 10/10 body and the on-cast trigger!
3. **Spawn & Scion Mana Engine:** Sacrificing Eldrazi Spawn and Scion tokens provides the exact {C}{C} required to pay for Ulalek's copy triggers on demand.
4. **Trigger Doubling Loops:** With *Echoes of Eternity* (6) or *Roaming Throne* (4), casting a single Eldrazi generates multiple Ulalek triggers, allowing exponential duplication of the entire stack.

## 🛠️ Card Explanations

### 👑 Commander (1 Card)
*   **Ulalek, Fused Atrocity:** 5-color Devoid commander; pays {C}{C} on casting an Eldrazi to copy all spells and abilities on the stack.

### ⚡ Fast Ramp & Cost Reducers (12 Cards)
*   **Sol Ring (1):** Premier fast artifact mana; taps for {C}{C} to immediately pay for Ulalek's trigger.
*   **Arcane Signet (2):** 2-mana rock tapping for any color in Ulalek's 5-color identity.
*   **Talisman of Curiosity (2):** Taps for {C} or Simic colors (G/U).
*   **Talisman of Dominance (2):** Taps for {C} or Dimir colors (U/B).
*   **Talisman of Impulse (2):** Taps for {C} or Gruul colors (R/G).
*   **Talisman of Resilience (2):** Taps for {C} or Golgari colors (B/G).
*   **Talisman of Progress (2):** Taps for {C} or Azorius colors (W/U).
*   **Herald's Horn (3):** Reduces Eldrazi creature costs by {1} and offers free card draw on upkeep.
*   **Urza's Incubator (3):** Reduces all Eldrazi creature spells by {2}.
*   **Forsaken Monument (5):** Doubles mana produced by colorless sources, grants +2/+2 to colorless creatures, and gains life on colorless cast.
*   **Morophon, the Boundless (7):** Reduces colored mana costs of Eldrazi to zero, effectively letting you cast Ulalek for {0}.
*   **Darksteel Monolith (8):** Indestructible artifact allowing you to cast one colorless spell from hand for {0} mana on each player's turn.

### 🔄 Stack Engines, Trigger Doublers & Copy Multipliers (6 Cards)
*   **Echoes of Eternity (6):** Kindred enchantment that copies all colorless spells and doubles all colorless triggered abilities.
*   **Roaming Throne (4):** Chosen type Eldrazi; doubles all Eldrazi triggered abilities including Ulalek's copy trigger.
*   **Glaring Fleshraker (3):** Generates a 0/1 Eldrazi Spawn (sacrifices for {C}) on every colorless cast and pings each opponent for 1.
*   **Eldrazi Displacer (3):** Instant-speed {2}{C} blink to protect your board, remove blockers, or reset ETB effects.
*   **Liberator, Urza's Battlethopter (3):** Grants flash to all colorless and artifact spells, enabling instant-speed Eldrazi drops and stack tricks.
*   **Strionic Resonator (2):** Taps for {2} to duplicate Ulalek's copy trigger or any on-cast Eldrazi ability.

### 📚 Card Advantage & Draw Engines (10 Cards)
*   **Kozilek's Unsealing (3):** Devoid enchantment; creates two 0/1 Spawn tokens ({C}{C}) when casting 4–6 CMC creatures (like Ulalek), and draws 3 cards when casting 7+ CMC Eldrazi.
*   **Sire of Stagnation (6):** Punishes opponents' land drops by exiling 2 cards and drawing you 2 cards each time.
*   **Mystic Forge (4):** Casts colorless cards directly from the top of your library and exiles unwanted top cards.
*   **Kozilek, the Great Distortion (10):** Refills your hand back up to 7 cards on cast and discards cards to counter spells.
*   **Kozilek, the Broken Reality (9):** Manifests 4 cards and draws 4 cards on cast, while granting +3/+2 to all colorless creatures.
*   **Up the Beanstalk (2):** Draws a card on ETB and whenever you cast a spell with mana value 5 or greater.
*   **Garruk's Uprising (3):** Draws a card on 4+ power creature entry and gives the entire team trample.
*   **Elemental Bond (3):** Draws a card whenever a creature with power 3 or greater enters the battlefield under your control.
*   **Rishkar's Expertise (6):** Draws cards equal to the greatest power on your board and casts a 5-mana spell for free.
*   **Ancient Stirrings (1):** 1-mana green cantrip digging 5 cards deep for any colorless card.

### 💥 High-Impact Eldrazi Titans & Payoffs (13 Cards)
*   **Ulamog, the Defiler (10):** On cast exiles half of target opponent's library; enters with +1/+1 counters equal to highest CMC in exile with Annihilator X and Ward—Sacrifice 2 permanents.
*   **Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger (10):** Exiles two target permanents on cast; indestructible 10/10 that exiles 20 cards on attack.
*   **Artisan of Kozilek (9):** On cast reanimates a creature from your graveyard; 10/10 with Annihilator 2.
*   **World Breaker (7):** On cast exiles target artifact, enchantment, or land; reach blocker with graveyard recursion.
*   **Benthic Anomaly (7):** On cast copies the highest power/toughness among creatures controlled by each opponent into one composite monster.
*   **Hideous Taskmaster (7):** On cast steals a creature from each opponent until end of turn, giving them trample, haste, and Annihilator 1.
*   **Azlask, the Swelling Scourge (3):** Accumulates experience counters on colorless creature death; 5-color activation turns Spawn/Scions into indestructible 8/8+ Annihilator 1 threats.
*   **Ulamog's Dreadsire (10):** Taps to create a 10/10 colorless Eldrazi creature token every turn.
*   **Elder Deep-Fiend (8):** Flash emerge creature that taps four target permanents on cast to clear blockers or tap down opposing mana.
*   **Cityscape Leveler (8):** Destroys a nonland permanent on cast and on each attack; 8/8 trample with unearth.
*   **Oblivion Sower (6):** On cast exiles the top 4 cards of an opponent's library and steals all lands from their exile onto your battlefield.
*   **Spawnbed Protector (7):** End step recurs an Eldrazi from your graveyard to hand and creates two 1/1 Eldrazi Scions.
*   **Zhulodok, Void Gorger (6):** Grants "Cascade, cascade" to all 7+ CMC colorless spells cast from your hand.

### 🛡️ Targeted Disruption, Protection & Utility (11 Cards)
*   **Eldritch Immunity (1):** 1-mana instant protection; overload {4}{C} gives your entire board protection from all colors at instant speed.
*   **Heroic Intervention (2):** Grants all permanents you control indestructible and hexproof.
*   **Warping Wail (2):** Flexible instant: exiles a 1-power creature, counters a sorcery, or creates an Eldrazi Scion.
*   **Null Elemental Blast (1):** 1-mana instant to destroy a multicolored permanent or counter a multicolored spell.
*   **Path to Exile (1):** Premier 1-mana instant creature exile; controller searches for a basic land tapped.
*   **Swords to Plowshares (1):** Premier 1-mana exile spot removal for problem creatures.
*   **Eldrazi Confluence (4):** Modal instant to exile a permanent, grant +3/-3, or create an Eldrazi Scion token.
*   **Lightning Greaves (2):** 0-equip equipment giving haste and shroud to Ulalek or Eldrazi titans.
*   **Swiftfoot Boots (2):** 1-equip equipment granting hexproof and haste.
*   **Wastescape Battlemage (3):** Modal Eldrazi kicker to bounce a creature and/or exile an artifact/enchantment.
*   **Chittering Dispatcher (3):** Myriad creature generating Eldrazi Spawn on leaving the battlefield.

### 🌪️ Mass Disruption & Finishers (7 Cards)
*   **Raise the Palisade (5):** 5-mana asymmetric tribal bounce; names Eldrazi to return all non-Eldrazi creatures to opponents' hands while leaving your army untouched.
*   **All Is Dust (7):** Asymmetric Eldrazi board wipe forcing all players to sacrifice all colored permanents.
*   **Selective Obliteration (5):** Color-based board wipe that spares your colorless permanents while wiping opponents' boards.
*   **Kozilek's Return (3):** 2-damage instant wipe that triggers from graveyard for a free 5-damage board sweep when casting a 7+ Eldrazi.
*   **Farewell (6):** Modal exile board wipe addressing artifacts, creatures, enchantments, and graveyards.
*   **Eldrazi Monument (5):** Grants flying, indestructible, and +1/+1 to your army in exchange for one creature sacrifice per upkeep.
*   **Return of the Wildspeaker (5):** Instant-speed choice between massive card draw based on highest power or a +3/+3 team overrun.

### 🔮 Additional Synergy Pieces (2 Cards)

*   **Ugin, the Ineffable (6):** Reduces colorless spells by {2}, generates 2/2 Spirit tokens that draw cards on death, and destroys colored permanents.
