## 1. Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

* **Final Placement:** **Bracket 3**
* **DTI Threat Index:** `33 / 96` (Score Floor: Bracket 3)
* **Clock Speed (Velocity Vector):** `16 / 48`
* **Opponent Stifle (Suppression Vector):** `15 / 40`
* **WotC Statutory Compliance:** 0 / 3 Game Changers (Floor: Bracket 2)

### Tactical Overview
Jin Sakai, Ghost of Tsushima operates as a Shape 2 (Amplifier) and Shape 4 (Pillar-Shifted Card Advantage) engine. By exploiting Jin Sakai's per-player solo attack trigger across all 3 opponents simultaneously, the deck grants up to three Saboteur creatures per combat either Unblockable (Ghost) to guarantee their combat-damage triggers or Double Strike (Standoff) to fire their triggers twice. Deliberately built with 0 Game Changers and a budget-conscious Esper mana base to anchor squarely in Low Bracket 3.

## 2. Operational Primer & Strategy

### Core Strategy
1. Turns 1-3 (Setup & Saboteur Deployment): Develop a 2-CMC mana rock (Talisman cycle, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone) or catch-up ramp body (Loyal Warhound) alongside a high-value 2-3 mana Saboteur (Malcolm, Daxos, Thief of Sanity, Cephalid Constable, Virtus the Veiled, Tymna, Breena).
2. Turn 4-5 (The Three-Front Standoff): Cast Jin Sakai, Ghost of Tsushima. Immediately attack up to three different opponents with one solo attacker each. Jin Sakai triggers immediately for each solo attacker without needing haste himself—choose Ghost (Unblockable) against opponents with blockers, or Standoff (Double Strike) against open opponents to double all combat-damage triggers.
3. Turns 6-8 (Life-Halving & First-Strike Ninjutsu Conversion): Use Double Strike attackers to deal First Strike combat damage (triggering abilities), then optionally Ninjutsu in Fallen Shinobi, Yuriko, or Mist-Syndicate Naga before the Regular Combat Damage step to double-dip triggers. Close the game by halving life totals via Virtus the Veiled and Quietus Spike, or overwhelming the board with True Conviction, Odric, Akroma's Will, and Vorpal Sword.

### Mulligan Priorities
* **Keep:** Hands with 3 lands (covering at least 2 of W/U/B), 1 two-mana ramp piece (Talisman, Signet, Sol Ring, Bauble, Loyal Warhound), and 1-2 early Saboteur or protection creatures (Mother of Runes, Pippin, Malcolm, Baleful Strix, Tymna).
* **Avoid:** Hands with multiple top-end payoffs, missing Blue/Black mana, or lacking early creatures to trigger Jin Sakai on Turn 4.

### Tactical Tips
- The 3-Player Solo Attack Rule: Jin Sakai checks 'if no other creatures are attacking THAT player.' In a 4-player pod, attacking Opponent A with Creature 1, Opponent B with Creature 2, and Opponent C with Creature 3 triggers Jin Sakai three separate times!
- The First-Strike Ninjutsu Double-Dip: When Jin Sakai grants an unblocked attacker Standoff (Double Strike), let it deal First Strike combat damage and resolve its saboteur trigger first. Then, still inside combat before the Regular Combat Damage step, activate Ninjutsu (Yuriko, Fallen Shinobi, Mist-Syndicate Naga)—the Ninja enters tapped and attacking and deals Regular Combat Damage in the exact same combat!
- Protection = Double Strike: Mother of Runes, Giver of Runes, and Pippin, Guard of the Citadel don't just protect Jin Sakai from removal—tapping them to give an attacker protection from an opponent's blocker color (or 'creatures' with Pippin) makes your attacker unblockable so Jin Sakai is free to choose Standoff (Double Strike)!

## 3. Strategic Weaknesses & Exploits

### Critical Vulnerabilities
- 🔴 Combat-step prevention and fog/pillowfort locks (Constant Mists, Glacial Chasm, Ghostly Prison, Propaganda, Ensnaring Bridge) shut down combat-damage triggers until removed via Anguished Unmaking, Void Rend, or Despark.

### Moderate Friction Points
- 🟡 Repeated creature board wipes delay the Saboteur critical mass, as the deck relies on maintaining 2-3 creatures on the battlefield to maximize split-combat value.
- 🟡 Budget Esper mana base runs Check, Reveal, Battle, and Tainted lands rather than fetch-shock-dual packages, occasionally introducing a tapped land on Turns 1-3.

### Minor Exploits
- ⚪ Incremental life loss from Talismans, Pain lands, Tymna the Weaver, and Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor (mitigated by Daxos, Tymna lifelink, True Conviction, and Akroma's Will).

## 4. Key Engine Anchors

- **[Jin Sakai, Ghost of Tsushima](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Jin%20Sakai%2C%20Ghost%20of%20Tsushima%22):** Command-zone card draw engine and 3-player split-combat amplifier granting Unblockable (Ghost) or Double Strike (Standoff).
- **[Cephalid Constable](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Cephalid%20Constable%22):** Bounces permanents (including lands) equal to combat damage dealt; unblockable or double strike from Jin Sakai turns it into a one-sided board wipe.
- **[Virtus the Veiled](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Virtus%20the%20Veiled%22):** 3-mana Assassin that cuts defending player's life in half on combat damage (quarters their life from 40 to 10 with Standoff Double Strike).
- **[Tymna the Weaver](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Tymna%20the%20Weaver%22):** Draws 3 cards every postcombat main phase alongside Jin Sakai's natural 3-opponent split attack pattern.
- **[Grim Hireling](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Grim%20Hireling%22):** Generates 2 Treasures per combat damage step per player—up to 12 Treasures per turn when three Double Strike creatures connect.
- **[Spark Double](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Spark%20Double%22):** Creates a non-legendary copy of Jin Sakai so every solo attacker receives BOTH Ghost (Unblockable) and Standoff (Double Strike) simultaneously.
- **[Fallen Shinobi](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Fallen%20Shinobi%22):** Premier Ninjutsu threat that exiles 2 cards per hit (4 cards with Double Strike) and casts them for free.
- **[Vorpal Sword](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Vorpal%20Sword%22):** Cheap deathtouch equipment whose {5}{B}{B}{B} activated ability pairs with Jin Sakai's Ghost mode for an unblockable one-hit player elimination.
- **[Pippin, Guard of the Citadel](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Pippin%2C%20Guard%20of%20the%20Citadel%22):** Protects Jin Sakai from spot removal or grants protection from creatures so a solo attacker can safely take Standoff Double Strike.
- **[Kamiz, Obscura Oculus](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Kamiz%2C%20Obscura%20Oculus%22):** Redundant command-zone lieutenant in the 99 that grants unblockable + Connive and Double Strike on attack.
- **[Reconnaissance Mission](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Reconnaissance%20Mission%22):** Draws a card on every combat damage hit—triggers twice per Double Strike creature across up to 3 opponents.
- **[Akroma's Will](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Akroma%27s%20Will%22):** Decisive finisher granting the entire team Double Strike, Flying, Lifelink, Indestructible, and Protection from all colors.
