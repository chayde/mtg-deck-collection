# Deck Guide: Mahadi, Emporium Master ("The Blood Market")

![Mahadi, Emporium Master](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/c/3/c3da2b1a-76c5-4d36-bdf8-025b86f61eb5.jpg?1783922689)

![Mahadi, Emporium Master](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/5/6/5647741c-7080-44dd-a642-169564a972b9.jpg)

### Commander: Mahadi, Emporium Master

> **Origin:** This deck is built as an aggressive, high-velocity **Treasure Aristocrats & Sacrifice Control** engine in Black-Red (Rakdos). It steps completely outside traditional Green ramp, converting symmetrical creature deaths into end-step Treasure piles that fuel game-ending X-spells and artifact-drain triggers.

## Commander Strategy
**Mahadi, Emporium Master**
*   **Archetype:** Rakdos ({B}{R}) Treasure Aristocrats / Sacrifice Control / Spellslinger Burn
*   **Bracket:** 3 (Upgraded — 1 Game Changer: *Bolas's Citadel*)
*   **Core Goal:** Deploy symmetrical edicts, board wipes, and recursive sacrifice fodder. At your end step, Mahadi triggers to award you **1 Treasure token for EVERY creature that died on the battlefield that turn** (yours and opponents'). You then sacrifice those Treasures to trigger artifact-drain pingers and fire off massive late-game X-spells.

### Three Interlocking Engines
1.  **Engine 1 — The Death-to-Treasure Market (Mahadi):** Mahadi turns every creature death across the table into a Treasure token at your end step. Edict creatures (**Plaguecrafter**, **Fleshbag Marauder**) net you 4 Treasures per cast, while board wipes (**Toxic Deluge**, **Blasphemous Act**) generate 10+ Treasures in a single turn.
2.  **Engine 2 — Artifact & Treasure Drain (The Aristocrats):** **Mayhem Devil**, **Mirkwood Bats**, **Marionette Master**, **Marionette Apprentice**, **Disciple of the Vault**, and **Nadier's Nightblade** turn the creation and sacrifice of Treasures directly into opponent life loss. Sacrificing 10 Treasures with Marionette Master deals **20 damage to every opponent**.
3.  **Engine 3 — Big Mana & Spellslinger Finishers:** Dumping stacks of 10–20 Treasures into **Torment of Hailfire** ($X=15$), **Exsanguinate**, **Blood for the Blood God!**, or enabling **Revel in Riches** for instant upkeep victory.

## 🛠️ Card Explanations

### 🗡️ Death Generators & Edicts (6 Cards)
*   **Plaguecrafter / Fleshbag Marauder / Demon's Disciple:** Symmetrical edicts that force each player to sacrifice a creature. With Mahadi out, casting a 3-drop edict generates 4 Treasures at end step (+1 mana profit!).
*   **Toxic Deluge:** Pay life to wipe the board of indestructible and high-toughness threats; generates a Treasure for every creature destroyed.
*   **Blasphemous Act:** 1-mana board wipe that converts an entire battlefield of creatures into a massive bank of Treasures.
*   **Szat's Will:** Symmetrical edict that forces opponents to sacrifice their highest-power creature, exiles all enemy graveyards, and creates Thrull tokens.

### 💰 Treasure Engines & Accelerators (10 Cards)
*   **Goldspan Dragon:** Makes all your Treasures tap for {2} mana instead of {1}, and creates extra Treasures whenever it attacks or is targeted.
*   **Warren Soultrader:** Free, instant-speed sacrifice outlet: pay 1 life and sacrifice a creature to create a Treasure token.
*   **Xorn:** Increases your Treasure output by +1 extra Treasure whenever you create Treasures.
*   **Professional Face-Breaker:** Creates Treasures on combat damage and allows you to sacrifice Treasures to impulse-draw from your library.
*   **Academy Manufactor:** Converts every single Treasure creation into 1 Treasure, 1 Clue, and 1 Food token!
*   **Sol Ring / Arcane Signet / Talisman of Indulgence / Rakdos Signet / Fellwar Stone:** Essential early Rakdos mana ramp.

### 🩸 Artifact & Treasure Drain Payoffs (31 Cards)
*   **Mayhem Devil:** Deals 1 damage to ANY target whenever ANY player sacrifices ANY permanent. Sacrificing 10 Treasures deals 10 free ping damage.
*   **Mirkwood Bats:** Drains opponents for 1 life whenever you CREATE or SACRIFICE a token. (5 Treasures created + 5 sacrificed = 10 damage to all opponents!).
*   **Marionette Master / Marionette Apprentice:** Drains opponents whenever artifacts or creatures enter the graveyard. Sacrificing 10 Treasures with Marionette Master deals 20 damage to every opponent.
*   **Disciple of the Vault / Nadier's Nightblade:** Drains opponents whenever artifacts or tokens leave the battlefield.
*   **Juri, Master of the Revue:** Gains a +1/+1 counter whenever ANY permanent is sacrificed. Fling a 15/15 Juri for lethal burn!
*   **Pitiless Plunderer / Pawn of Ulamog / Sifter of Skulls:** Secondary death-to-token engines that stack with Mahadi.
*   **Reassembling Skeleton / Bloodghast / Nether Traitor / Gravecrawler:** Endless recursive fodder to sacrifice every turn cycle.
*   **Carrion Feeder / Viscera Seer / Goblin Bombardment / Phyrexian Altar:** Free, instant-speed sacrifice outlets.
*   **Revel in Riches:** Alternate win condition that creates Treasures on enemy deaths and wins the game on your upkeep if you control 10+ Treasures.
*   **There and Back Again:** Saga that ramp a Mountain, tempts with the Ring, and creates a 6/6 Smaug token that drops **14 Treasures** when it dies!
*   **Torment of Hailfire / Exsanguinate / Crackle with Power:** Game-ending X-spells powered by Mahadi's Treasure reserves.

### 🎴 Card Advantage & Draw (12 Cards)
*   **Bolas's Citadel:** [Game Changer] Look at the top card of your library anytime and cast spells paying life instead of mana. Sacrificing 10 nonland artifacts deals 10 damage to each opponent.
*   **Blood for the Blood God!:** 11-CMC instant that costs {1} less for each creature that died this turn. Deals 8 damage to all opponents, draws 8 cards, and exiles your hand!
*   **Skullclamp:** Equip to 1-toughness fodder creatures to draw 2 cards per death.
*   **Faithless Looting / Unexpected Windfall / Big Score / Demand Answers / Deadly Dispute / Corrupted Conviction / Night's Whisper / Morbid Opportunist / Species Specialist:** Premier Rakdos card selection, rummaging, and Treasure-generating draw.

### 🛡️ Targeted Disruption & Interaction (12 Cards)
*   **Deflecting Swat:** Free spell redirected protection when Mahadi is on the battlefield.
*   **Deadly Rollick / Snuff Out:** Free targeted creature removal.
*   **Terminate / Bedevil / Chaos Warp / Bitter Triumph / Abrade / Feed the Swarm / Vandalblast / Tibalt's Trickery / Rakdos Charm:** Versatile spot removal, counter-magic, artifact destruction, and graveyard exile.

### 🧱 Mana Base (38 Cards)
*   **Command Tower / Exotic Orchard / Luxury Suite / Blood Crypt / Bloodstained Mire / Polluted Delta / Arid Mesa / Scalding Tarn / Verdant Catacombs / Marsh Flats / Wooded Foothills / Haunted Ridge / Dragonskull Summit / Smoldering Marsh / Bojuka Bog / High Market / Phyrexian Tower / Takenuma, Abandoned Mire / Mount Doom / Rogue's Passage / Foreboding Ruins / Tainted Peak / Graven Cairns / Myriad Landscape / 7 Swamp / 6 Mountain**
