# Deck Guide: Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder ("The Maelstrom Engine")

![Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/c/2/c219dd22-433c-4f6a-a0c4-fae12239291c.jpg)

### Commander: Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder

> **Origin:** Built as a premier **{U}{B}{R}{G} Non-White Cascade & Exile-Storm Engine**. Following combat damage from Yidris, every spell cast from hand gains Cascade, turning single card investments into cascading torrents of free spells, lethal exile burn, and game-ending stack multiplication.

---

## Commander Strategy
**Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder**
*   **Archetype:** 4-Color ({U}{B}{R}{G}) Combat Cascade / Exile-Cast Storm / Delve Velocity
*   **Bracket:** 3 (Upgraded — 2 Game Changers: *Cyclonic Rift*, *Jeska's Will*)
*   **Core Goal:** Deploy Yidris with haste or unblockable protection to connect for combat damage. Once connected, leverage post-combat main phase cascade triggers to cheat 0-mana suspend jackpots (*Ancestral Vision*, *Profane Tutor*, *Wheel of Fate*), discount high-CMC delve spells (*Dig Through Time*, *Blasphemous Act*), and incinerate opponents via exile-cast burn engines (*Passionate Archaeologist*, *Keeper of Secrets*, *Flaming Tyrannosaurus*).

### Three Interlocking Engines
1.  **Engine 1 — Combat Connection & Trigger Multipliers (The Spark):** Yidris requires combat damage to unlock cascading value. We ensure connections via **Whispersilk Cloak**, **Brotherhood Regalia** (unblockable + ward {2}), **Lightning Greaves**, **Swiftfoot Boots**, **Shizo, Death's Storehouse**, and **The Black Gate**. Trigger doublers like **Harmonic Prodigy** (doubles Wizard combat triggers!), **Felix Five-Boots**, **Lizard Blades** (Double Strike deals combat damage twice $\rightarrow$ stacks two cascade instances!), and land untappers **Bear Umbra** and **Sword of Feast and Famine** multiply post-combat mana and cascade density exponentially.
2.  **Engine 2 — 0-Drop Suspend Jackpots & Delve Cheats:** Cascade ignores mana costs on exiled cards. Low-CMC cascade triggers guarantee hitting 0-mana powerhouses: **Ancestral Vision** (draw 3), **Profane Tutor** (demonic tutor for free), **Wheel of Fate** (hand refill), and **Inevitable Betrayal** (steal opponent's best creature). Meanwhile, high-CMC cost reducers like **Dig Through Time** ({U}{U} delve $\rightarrow$ cascades for 7) and **Blasphemous Act** ({R} wipe $\rightarrow$ cascades for 8) cheat maximum mana value.
3.  **Engine 3 — Exile-Cast Stack Multiplication & Lethal Burn:** Casting spells from exile triggers devastating table-wide payoffs: **Passionate Archaeologist** (burns target opponent for spell CMC), **Keeper of Secrets** (deals direct damage equal to spell CMC), **Nalfeshnee** (copies every spell cast from exile!), **Flaming Tyrannosaurus** (+1/+1 counters & 3 damage per exile cast + death trigger), **Prosper, Tome-Bound** (creates Treasure per exile cast), **Wild-Magic Sorcerer** (grants additional cascade), and **Averna, the Chaos Bloom** (ramps any cascaded lands directly into play tapped!).

## 🛠️ Card Explanations

### ⚡ Combat Evasion, Multipliers & Protection (9 Cards)
*   **Harmonic Prodigy:** **S-TIER YIDRIS MULTIPLIER ($4.91).** Yidris is an Ogre Wizard — Harmonic Prodigy causes Yidris's combat damage trigger to trigger an additional time, granting TWO instances of cascade to every spell cast from hand!
*   **Felix Five-Boots:** **COMBAT TRIGGER DOUBLER ($4.22).** Menace + ward {2}. Copies combat damage triggers of creatures you control, doubling Yidris's cascade enablement.
*   **Lizard Blades:** Reconfigure {2} equipment giving Double Strike; dealing combat damage in first strike and regular damage stacks two cascade triggers on all spells.
*   **Brotherhood Regalia:** Grants unblockable and ward {2} to legendary creatures for {1} equip cost.
*   **Whispersilk Cloak:** Makes Yidris unblockable with shroud, guaranteeing combat connection.
*   **Lightning Greaves / Swiftfoot Boots:** Haste enablers and targeted removal protection.
*   **Bear Umbra:** Totem armor aura granting +2/+2 and untapping all lands upon attack to double mana for Main Phase 2.
*   **Sword of Feast and Famine:** Protects from Black and Green, forces discard, and untaps all lands on combat hit.

### 🎰 Cascade & Exile-Cast Finishers (12 Cards)
*   **Passionate Archaeologist:** **PREMIER EXILE BURN ENGINE.** Causes your commander to deal damage equal to mana value to target opponent whenever you cast a spell from exile.
*   **Keeper of Secrets:** 6/4 Haste First Strike Demon that deals damage equal to mana value to target opponent whenever a spell is cast from exile.
*   **Nalfeshnee:** 4/6 Flying Demon that copies every spell cast from exile, doubling your cascaded spells!
*   **Flaming Tyrannosaurus:** Paradox dinosaur that puts +1/+1 counters and deals 3 damage on exile cast, and deals power to all opponents on death.
*   **Prosper, Tome-Bound:** Deathtouch engine generating Treasure tokens whenever you cast spells from exile.
*   **Wild-Magic Sorcerer:** Grants cascade to the first spell cast from exile each turn, chaining cascades deeper.
*   **Averna, the Chaos Bloom:** Turns cascade land whiffs into free tapped land ramp on the battlefield!
*   **Imoti, Celebrant of Bounty:** Gives all 6+ CMC spells cascade and cascades on entry.
*   **Maelstrom Wanderer:** 7/5 haste anthem for the whole team with built-in double cascade.
*   **Apex Devastator:** 10/10 bomb with quadruple cascade (casts 4 free spells from library).
*   **Etali, Primal Conqueror:** Steals and casts a free spell from every player's library on ETB, then transforms into an 11/11 indestructible poison finisher.
*   **Chimil, the Inner Sun:** Makes spells uncounterable and discovers 5 for free at every end step.

### 🎯 0-Mana Suspend Jackpots & Tutors (3 Cards)
*   **Ancestral Vision:** 0-mana suspend spell that draws 3 cards immediately when hit off any low-CMC cascade.
*   **Wheel of Fate:** 0-mana suspend wheel that discards hands and draws 7 cards to reload the cascade engine.
*   **Profane Tutor:** 0-mana suspend tutor that searches library for any card with zero mana investment.

### 🔮 Card Advantage & Top-Deck Sculpting (12 Cards)
*   **Brainstorm:** Instant-speed topdeck sculptor; puts 2 expensive cards or cascade targets onto top of library.
*   **Ponder:** Topdeck sorting and card selection.
*   **Sensei's Divining Top:** Repeatable 1-mana library reordering and emergency draw.
*   **Sylvan Library:** Top-3 card selection every draw step with optional life-for-cards payment.
*   **Up the Beanstalk:** Draws a card on ETB and every time a 5+ CMC spell is cast (synergizes with cascade and delve).
*   **Rishkar's Expertise:** Draws 5+ cards off Yidris's power and casts a free 5-CMC spell from hand.
*   **Return of the Wildspeaker:** Instant-speed burst draw based on greatest power or +3/+3 team overrun.
*   **Dig Through Time:** Delve instant looking at top 7 cards (CMC 8 cascades for a 7-drop for only {U}{U}).
*   **Windfall:** Refuels hand to the highest hand size at the table while disrupting opponent hands.
*   **Valakut Awakening:** MDFC land on back face; redraws hand + 1 card at instant speed.
*   **Jeska's Will:** **GAME CHANGER #2.** Generates massive red burst mana and impulse exiles top 3 cards to chain spells.

### 🛡️ Targeted Disruption & Interaction (12 Cards)
*   **Deflecting Swat:** Free redirect spell when commander is on board.
*   **Deadly Rollick:** Free instant-speed creature exile when commander is on board.
*   **Heroic Intervention:** Instant-speed hexproof and indestructible protection for the entire board.
*   **Delayed Blast Fireball:** One-sided wipe dealing 5 damage to all opponents and their creatures when cast from exile (cascade/foretell).
*   **Assassin's Trophy / Beast Within / Chaos Warp / Putrefy:** Universal spot removal for any permanent type.
*   **Flame of Anor:** Tri-modal instant (draw 2, destroy artifact, deal 5 damage) with all modes unlocked by Yidris (Wizard!).
*   **Rakdos Charm:** Modal graveyard wipe, artifact destruction, or wide-token burn punisher.
*   **Counterspell:** Hard 2-mana countermagic.
*   **Sink into Stupor:** MDFC untapped blue land or instant spell/permanent bounce.

### 💣 Mass Disruption & Cascade Wipes (6 Cards)
*   **Cyclonic Rift:** **GAME CHANGER #1.** Premier asymmetrical board reset overloaded for {6}{U}.
*   **Blasphemous Act:** 13-damage board wipe costing {R} with full board that cascades for an 8-drop!
*   **Toxic Deluge:** Scalable life-payment wipe that bypasses Indestructible.
*   **Volcanic Torrent:** Cascade board wipe dealing damage to opponents' boards equal to total spells cast this turn.
*   **In Garruk's Wake:** One-sided 9-CMC creature and planeswalker wipe that cascades for an 8-drop.
*   **Baral's Expertise:** Bounces 3 creatures/artifacts and casts a free 4-drop spell from hand.

### 💎 Mana Ramp & Acceleration (10 Cards)
*   **Sol Ring / Arcane Signet / Fellwar Stone:** Essential artifact mana rocks.
*   **Birds of Paradise / Ignoble Hierarch / Delighted Halfling / Bloom Tender:** 1-drop and 2-drop multi-color mana dorks; Halfling makes Yidris uncounterable.
*   **Farseek / Nature's Lore / Three Visits:** 2-mana land ramp spells fetching shock lands and triomes into play untapped.

### 🧱 Mana Base (38 Lands)
*   **Command Tower / Exotic Orchard / Reflecting Pool / Plaza of Heroes:** 4-color rainbow fixing. Plaza protects Yidris with hexproof/indestructible.
*   **10 Fetch Lands (Polluted Delta, Scalding Tarn, Wooded Foothills, Misty Rainforest, Verdant Catacombs, Bloodstained Mire, Marsh Flats, Arid Mesa, Windswept Heath, Prismatic Vista):** Perfect color fixing and graveyard fuel for delve spells.
*   **6 Shock Lands (Breeding Pool, Blood Crypt, Watery Grave, Stomping Ground, Steam Vents, Overgrown Tomb):** Fetchable dual lands entering untapped.
*   **4 Triomes (Ketria Triome, Zagoth Triome, Ziatora's Proving Ground, Xander's Lounge):** Fetchable 3-color lands with cycling.
*   **6 Bond Lands (Morphic Pool, Rejuvenating Springs, Training Center, Undergrowth Stadium, Luxury Suite, Spire Garden):** Untapped multiplayer dual lands.
*   **Utility & Evasion Lands:** **Rogue's Passage** (unblockable), **Shizo, Death's Storehouse** (fear), **The Black Gate** (unblockable for 3 life), **Reliquary Tower** (no max hand size).
*   **4 Basic Lands (1 Forest, 1 Island, 1 Swamp, 1 Mountain):** Basic land protection against non-basic hate.
