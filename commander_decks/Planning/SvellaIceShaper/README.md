## Strategy Summary

Svella acts as a self-contained ramp foundry and topdeck cheat engine in the command zone. For {3}, she manufactures permanent {T}: Add 1 mana **Icy Manaliths**, steadily growing your mana baseline without consuming card slots. Once she reaches 8 mana, her second ability lets you look 4 cards deep and **cast** huge spells for free at instant speed.

With artifact untappers (**Unwinding Clock**, **Clock of Omens**, **Sting, the Glinting Dagger**) and ability copiers (**Illusionist's Bracers**, **Battlemage's Bracers**, **Rings of Brighthearth**), Svella can be activated multiple times per turn cycle, rapidly flooding the battlefield with Eldrazi Titans, Cascade bombs, and game-altering threats.
