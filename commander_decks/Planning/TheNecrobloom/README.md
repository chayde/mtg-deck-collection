## Strategic Summary
Combines a 41-land base (featuring snow basics, fetchlands, cycling lands, and horizon lands) with The Necrobloom's built-in **Field of the Dead** and **Dredge 2** abilities. The deck uses cycling and sac-lands to mill aggressively into the graveyard, while incremental recursion (*Crucible of Worlds*, *Ramunap Excavator*, *Six*) and mass detonators (*Splendid Reclamation*, *Lumra, Bellow of the Woods*, *Aftermath Analyst*, *Scapeshift*) convert stocked graveyards into massive 2/2 Zombie armies and lethal drain via *Syr Konrad, the Grim* and *Mirkwood Bats*.
