## Strategic Summary
Operates as an inexhaustible **Go-Wide Zombie Apocalypse Engine**. Utilizes The Necrobloom as a literal Field of the Dead in the command zone, creating 2/2 black Zombie creature tokens on every land drop once 7 differently named lands are controlled. Stacks with [Field of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Field+of+the+Dead") and token doublers ([Anointed Procession](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Anointed+Procession"), [Mondrak, Glory Dominus](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mondrak,+Glory+Dominus")) to create 2–4 Zombies per land drop.

Features explosive mass land bursts ([Scapeshift](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Scapeshift"), [Splendid Reclamation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Splendid+Reclamation"), [Lumra](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Lumra,+Bellow+of+the+Woods"), [Aftermath Analyst](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Aftermath+Analyst"), [Awaken the Woods](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Awaken+the+Woods")) that drop 6–12 lands at once to spawn 12–24 Zombies in a single turn. Converts mass landfall entries into one-sided board wipes with [Noxious Ghoul](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Noxious+Ghoul") (giving non-Zombies -1/-1 per entering Zombie), direct table drain with [Wayward Servant](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Wayward+Servant"), unblockable combat strikes via [Zombie Master](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zombie+Master") + [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Urborg,+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth") Swampwalk, and lethal overrun swings with [Beastmaster Ascension](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Beastmaster+Ascension"), [Akroma's Will](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Akroma's+Will"), and [Undead Warchief](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Undead+Warchief").

Card advantage is powered by token-fed engines ([Skullclamp](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Skullclamp"), [Idol of Oblivion](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Idol+of+Oblivion"), [Braids, Arisen Nightmare](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Braids,+Arisen+Nightmare"), [Cryptbreaker](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cryptbreaker"), [Black Market Connections](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Black+Market+Connections"), [Shamanic Revelation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Shamanic+Revelation")) to ensure the pilot maintains a full hand without falling into dredge traps.
