# Deck Guide: The Necrobloom — "Dredge & Field of the Dead Engine"

![The Necrobloom](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/8/f/8fcf68cf-0dac-4b29-90d5-c18c685182e6.jpg?1783911249)

## Commander Strategy
**The Necrobloom**
*   **Archetype:** Abzan ({W}{B}{G}) Lands / Dredge / Graveyard Recursion Midrange
*   **Bracket:** 3 (Optimized Power / High Synergy — Winning Turns 6–8)
*   **Game Changers (1/3):** [Crop Rotation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Crop+Rotation") ({G})
*   **Core Goal:** Combine a dense 41-land base with built-in dredge and recurring graveyard engines. The Necrobloom provides a permanent **Field of the Dead** in the command zone while turning all land cards in your graveyard into **Dredge 2** fuel. By cycling lands, sacrificing fetches, and replacing card draws with dredge, you rapidly dump lands into the graveyard, sculpt your hand, and set up mass land-reanimation spells (*Splendid Reclamation*, *Lumra, Bellow of the Woods*, *Aftermath Analyst*, *Scapeshift*) to put 10–20 lands onto the battlefield in a single turn, creating an overwhelming army of 2/2 Zombies while draining opponents with *Syr Konrad* and *Mirkwood Bats*.

### 🚜 Piloting the Engine
1.  **Turns 1–2 (Mana Acceleration & Foundation):** Deploy 1-drop mana dorks (*Birds of Paradise*, *Delighted Halfling*) or land ramp (*Nature's Lore*, *Three Visits*, *Farseek*) while cracking fetchlands to set up color fixing and stock the graveyard with your first dredge targets.
2.  **Turns 3–4 (Commander Deployment & Engine Online):** Cast **The Necrobloom** on Turn 3 or 4. Begin leveraging cycling lands (*Barren Moor*, *Tranquil Thicket*, *Secluded Steppe*) or Horizon lands to mill 2 cards on every draw. Deploy incremental recursion engines (*Crucible of Worlds*, *Ramunap Excavator*, *Six*) to play fetchlands directly from the graveyard, guaranteeing 2+ land drops and tokens per turn.
3.  **Turns 5–7 (The Mass Landfall Detonation):** Once 8–15 lands sit in your graveyard from dredge and sac-lands, resolve a mass reanimation bomb (*Splendid Reclamation*, *Lumra, Bellow of the Woods*, *Aftermath Analyst*, or *Scapeshift*). All lands enter simultaneously, generating 10–20 2/2 Zombie tokens, triggering massive drain via *Syr Konrad, the Grim* and *Mirkwood Bats*, and unlocking 15+ mana to overwhelm the table.

## 🛠️ Card Explanations

### 👑 Commander (1 Card)
*   **The Necrobloom:** {1}{W}{B}{G} 2/7 Plant. Grants all land cards in your graveyard Dredge 2; creates 0/1 Plants on landfall, or 2/2 Zombies if you control 7+ differently named lands. The central engine and token win condition.

### ⚡ Game Changers (1 Card — Bracket 3 Max 3)
*   **Crop Rotation:** {G} instant. Sacrifices a land as an additional cost to tutor ANY land card directly onto the battlefield untapped at instant speed; feeds dredge while tutoring silver bullets like *Bojuka Bog*, *Talon Gates of Madara*, or *Dakmor Salvage*. (Official Game Changer: 1/3).

### ♻️ Lands-from-Graveyard & Incremental Recursion (8 Cards)
*Reusable engines that turn your graveyard into a second hand.*
*   **Crucible of Worlds:** {3} artifact. Allows playing lands directly from your graveyard; enables infinite fetchland cycles and continuous landfall triggers.
*   **Ramunap Excavator:** {2}{G} 2/3 Snake Cleric. Creature-based Crucible of Worlds that is easily tutorable and reanimatable.
*   **Conduit of Worlds:** {4} artifact. Plays lands from the graveyard and lets you cast a nonland permanent from the graveyard once per turn.
*   **Six:** {2}{G} 2/4 Treefolk. Attacks to mill 3 and recover a land; grants all nonland permanents in your graveyard Retrace on your turn by discarding lands.
*   **Life from the Loam:** {1}{G} sorcery. Returns up to 3 lands from your graveyard to hand with natural Dredge 3; the premier lands engine card in MTG.
*   **Sevinne's Reclamation:** {2}{W} sorcery. Returns a permanent with CMC <= 3 from graveyard to battlefield; flashback copies itself to recur two permanents.
*   **Dread Return:** {2}{B}{B} sorcery. Reanimates any creature from your graveyard; flashback sacrifices 3 tokens (trivial with Necrobloom) to cast for 0 mana.
*   **Timeless Witness:** {3}{G} 2/1 Human Shaman. Returns any card from graveyard to hand on ETB; features Eternalize to re-cast itself from the graveyard.

### 💥 Mass Land Reanimation & Detonators (4 Cards)
*Game-ending spells that return all milled lands from the graveyard to the battlefield simultaneously.*
*   **Splendid Reclamation:** {3}{G} sorcery. Returns all land cards from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped; creates an instant army of 10+ Zombies with Necrobloom.
*   **Aftermath Analyst:** {1}{G} 1/3 Elf Detective. Mills 3 on ETB; activates for {3}{G} and sacrifices itself to return all lands from graveyard to the battlefield tapped.
*   **Lumra, Bellow of the Woods:** {4}{G}{G} */* Elemental Bear. Vigilance, reach; power/toughness equals land count. Mills 4 and returns all lands from graveyard to the battlefield tapped on ETB.
*   **Scapeshift:** {2}{G}{G} sorcery. Sacrifices any number of lands and tutors that many onto the battlefield tapped; instantly triggers 7+ unique names and generates an entire board of Zombies.

### 🌍 Additional Land Drops & Velocity (6 Cards)
*Enablers that convert lands in hand into explosive multi-landfall turns.*
*   **Azusa, Lost but Seeking:** {2}{G} 1/2 Human Monk. Allows 2 additional land drops every turn; supercharges landfall output and graveyard land loops.
*   **Exploration:** {G} enchantment. Allows 1 additional land drop every turn for just 1 mana.
*   **Dryad of the Ilysian Grove:** {2}{G} 2/4 Nymph Dryad. Allows an additional land drop and makes all lands every basic type for flawless mana fixing.
*   **The Gitrog Monster:** {3}{B}{G} 6/6 Frog Horror. Grants an additional land drop; draws a card whenever one or more land cards enter your graveyard from anywhere.
*   **Oracle of Mul Daya:** {3}{G} 2/2 Elf Shaman. Grants an additional land drop and plays lands directly off the top of your library revealed.
*   **Wayward Swordtooth:** {2}{G} 5/5 Dinosaur. Grants an additional land drop; easily achieves City's Blessing (Ascend) with token swarms.

### 🚜 Land Tutors & Sacrifice Outlets (5 Cards)
*Cards that voluntarily send lands to the graveyard while searching for crucial utility lands.*
*   **Elvish Reclaimer:** {G} 1/2 Elf Warrior. Grows to 3/4 with 3+ lands in graveyard; taps to sacrifice a land and tutor any land card onto the battlefield tapped.
*   **Wight of the Reliquary:** {B}{G} 2/2 Zombie Knight. Grows with creatures in graveyard; taps to sacrifice another creature (tokens) and tutor any land onto the battlefield tapped.
*   **Sylvan Safekeeper:** {G} 1/1 Human Wizard. Sacrifices a land at instant speed for 0 mana to grant target creature shroud, protecting Necrobloom while triggering land death.
*   **Knight of the Reliquary:** {1}{G}{W} 2/2 Human Knight. Grows +1/+1 for each land in your graveyard; taps and sacrifices a Forest or Plains to tutor any land into play untapped.
*   **Harrow:** {2}{G} instant. Sacrifices a land as an additional cost to search for two basic lands and put them onto the battlefield untapped at instant speed.

### 💀 Landfall Payoffs & Drain Finishers (6 Cards)
*Converts land drops, creature generation, and self-milling into lethal damage.*
*   **Scute Swarm:** {2}{G} 1/1 Insect. Exponential token generator that clones itself on every landfall trigger once you control 6+ lands.
*   **Felidar Retreat:** {3}{W} enchantment. Modal landfall trigger that either creates 2/2 Cat Beasts or puts a +1/+1 counter on all creatures and grants vigilance.
*   **Avenger of Zendikar:** {5}{G}{G} 5/5 Elemental. Creates a 0/1 Plant for every land you control on ETB, then puts a +1/+1 counter on all Plants on every subsequent landfall.
*   **Syr Konrad, the Grim:** {3}{B}{B} 5/4 Human Knight. Deals 1 damage to each opponent whenever a creature dies, leaves your graveyard, or a creature card is milled via Dredge.
*   **Mirkwood Bats:** {3}{B} 2/3 Bat. Drains each opponent for 1 life whenever you create a token (Zombies/Plants) or sacrifice a token.
*   **Tireless Provisioner:** {2}{G} 3/2 Elf Scout. Landfall generates a Treasure or Food token, bridging mana ramp and life preservation.

### 📖 Card Advantage & Dredge Velocity (7 Cards)
*Engines that draw multiple cards per turn to provide repeated dredge replacement triggers.*
*   **Sylvan Library:** {1}{G} enchantment. In your draw step, draw 2 additional cards; allows replacing any of the 3 draws with Dredge 2 to mill up to 6 cards per turn.
*   **Morbid Opportunist:** {1}{B}{B} 1/3 Human Rogue. Draws a card the first time a creature dies each turn; triggers on opponent turns and off token chump blocks.
*   **Skullclamp:** {1} artifact. Equips to 0/1 Plant tokens for {1} to kill them immediately and draw 2 cards (or dredge twice).
*   **Greater Good:** {2}{G}{G} enchantment. Sacrifices large creatures (like *The Gitrog Monster*, *Lumra*, or pumped Knights) to draw cards and discard 3, supercharging dredge.
*   **Shamanic Revelation:** {3}{G}{G} sorcery. Draws a card for each creature you control and gains 4 life for each creature with power 4 or greater; massive reload with Zombie armies.
*   **Painful Truths:** {2}{B} sorcery. Converge draws 3 cards and loses 3 life for 3 mana; provides 3 consecutive draw/dredge triggers.
*   **Braids, Arisen Nightmare:** {1}{B}{B} 3/3 Nightmare. Sacrifices an artifact, creature, or land at end step; opponents must sacrifice the same type or you draw a card and drain 2 life.

### 🌲 Mana Dorks & Early Ramp (8 Cards)
*Fast mana acceleration ensuring consistent Turn 3 Necrobloom deployment.*
*   **Sol Ring:** {1} artifact. Premier fast mana staple; taps for {C}{C}.
*   **Arcane Signet:** {2} artifact. Two-mana mana rock fixing all three Abzan colors.
*   **Birds of Paradise:** {G} 0/1 flying. Turn 1 mana dork tapping for {W}, {B}, or {G}.
*   **Delighted Halfling:** {G} 1/2. Taps for {C} or any color for legendary spells, making The Necrobloom and The Gitrog Monster uncounterable.
*   **Deathrite Shaman:** {B/G} 1/2 Elf Shaman. Taps to exile lands from graveyards for mana; provides targeted graveyard exile and life drain.
*   **Nature's Lore:** {1}{G} sorcery. Tutors an Overgrown Tomb, Temple Garden, or basic Forest onto the battlefield untapped.
*   **Three Visits:** {1}{G} sorcery. Functional duplicate of *Nature's Lore*.
*   **Farseek:** {1}{G} sorcery. Tutors a Plains or Swamp shock/dual land onto the battlefield tapped.

### 🗡️ Targeted Removal & Instant Disruption (8 Cards)
*Flexible interaction answering opposing threats, hate pieces, and stax.*
*   **Swords to Plowshares:** {W} instant. Premier 1-mana unconditional creature exile.
*   **Path to Exile:** {W} instant. Efficient 1-mana instant creature exile.
*   **Assassin's Trophy:** {B}{G} instant. Destroys any target permanent an opponent controls at instant speed.
*   **Anguished Unmaking:** {1}{W}{B} instant. Exiles any nonland permanent at instant speed at the cost of 3 life.
*   **Tear Asunder:** {1}{G} instant. Exiles an artifact or enchantment for 2 mana, or kicks for {B} to exile any nonland permanent.
*   **Beast Within:** {2}{G} instant. Destroys any target permanent, leaving behind a 3/3 Beast.
*   **Stroke of Midnight:** {2}{W} instant. Destroys target nonland permanent, leaving behind a 1/1 Human token.
*   **Deadly Rollick:** {3}{B} instant. Free 0-mana instant creature exile while The Necrobloom is on the battlefield.

### 🧹 Board Wipes & Mass Disruption (4 Cards)
*Reset buttons designed to break parity with graveyard recursion and token swarms.*
*   **Toxic Deluge:** {2}{B} sorcery. Flexible -X/-X sweeper that bypasses indestructible by paying life.
*   **Culling Ritual:** {2}{B}{G} sorcery. Destroys all nonland permanents CMC <= 2 and adds {B} or {G} for each permanent destroyed; explosive mana swing that cleans up opposing dorks and tokens.
*   **Pernicious Deed:** {1}{B}{G} enchantment. Scalable enchantment wipe that sweeps creatures and artifacts without touching lands.
*   **Damnation:** {2}{B}{B} sorcery. Clean, unconditional 4-mana creature destruction.

### 🏔️ Land Base (41 Lands)
*Carefully calibrated with 41 differently named lands to reliably trigger The Necrobloom's 7-different-names Zombie threshold.*
*   **Forest:** Basic land ({G}).
*   **Snow-Covered Forest:** Snow basic land ({G}); counts as a distinct name for Zombie threshold.
*   **Plains:** Basic land ({W}).
*   **Snow-Covered Plains:** Snow basic land ({W}); counts as a distinct name for Zombie threshold.
*   **Swamp:** Basic land ({B}).
*   **Snow-Covered Swamp:** Snow basic land ({B}); counts as a distinct name for Zombie threshold.
*   **Verdant Catacombs:** Sacrifices to fetch a Swamp or Forest; feeds graveyard recursion.
*   **Windswept Heath:** Sacrifices to fetch a Forest or Plains.
*   **Marsh Flats:** Sacrifices to fetch a Plains or Swamp.
*   **Prismatic Vista:** Sacrifices to tutor any basic or snow basic land untapped.
*   **Fabled Passage:** Sacrifices to tutor any basic or snow basic land untapped (with 4+ lands).
*   **Barren Moor:** Enters tapped; cycles for {B} to trigger instant Dredge 2 self-mill.
*   **Tranquil Thicket:** Enters tapped; cycles for {G} to trigger instant Dredge 2 self-mill.
*   **Secluded Steppe:** Enters tapped; cycles for {W} to trigger instant Dredge 2 self-mill.
*   **Polluted Mire:** Enters tapped; cycles for {2} to trigger Dredge 2.
*   **Slippery Karst:** Enters tapped; cycles for {2} to trigger Dredge 2.
*   **Dakmor Salvage:** Enters tapped; has innate Dredge 2 even if Necrobloom is temporarily removed.
*   **Talon Gates of Madara:** Utility gate that phases out any target creature on ETB; can be put into play from hand for {4} or fetched with Crop Rotation.
*   **Urza's Cave:** Sacrifices itself for {3} to tutor ANY land card directly onto the battlefield tapped.
*   **Bojuka Bog:** Enters tapped; exiles target player's entire graveyard on ETB.
*   **Ghost Quarter:** Taps for {C}; sacrifices to destroy target problematic land (Cradle, Coffers, Nykthos).
*   **Demolition Field:** Taps for {C}; destroys a nonbasic land while giving both players a basic land.
*   **High Market:** Taps for {C}; sacrifices a creature for 1 life, enabling death triggers.
*   **Phyrexian Tower:** Taps for {C}, or sacrifices a creature to add {B}{B}.
*   **Horizon Canopy:** Taps for {G} or {W}; sacrifices to draw a card (or dredge a land).
*   **Nurturing Peatland:** Taps for {B} or {G}; sacrifices to draw a card (or dredge a land).
*   **Silent Clearing:** Taps for {W} or {B}; sacrifices to draw a card (or dredge a land).
*   **Overgrown Tomb:** Swamp Forest shockland; enters untapped for 2 life.
*   **Temple Garden:** Forest Plains shockland; enters untapped for 2 life.
*   **Godless Shrine:** Plains Swamp shockland; enters untapped for 2 life.
*   **Indatha Triome:** Tri-land ({W}{B}{G}) with Forest Plains Swamp types; cycles for {3}.
*   **Undergrowth Stadium:** Enters untapped in multiplayer; adds {B} or {G}.
*   **Vault of Champions:** Enters untapped in multiplayer; adds {W} or {B}.
*   **Bountiful Promenade:** Enters untapped in multiplayer; adds {G} or {W}.
*   **Command Tower:** Taps for any color in commander's color identity.
*   **Exotic Orchard:** Taps for any color that an opponent's land could produce.
*   **Reflecting Pool:** Taps for any type of mana that a land you control could produce.
*   **Llanowar Wastes:** Pain land; adds {C}, or {B}/{G} for 1 life.
*   **Caves of Koilos:** Pain land; adds {C}, or {W}/{B} for 1 life.
*   **Brushland:** Pain land; adds {C}, or {G}/{W} for 1 life.
*   **Shifting Woodland:** Enters tapped unless you control a Forest; Delirium {2}{G}{G} turns it into a copy of any permanent in your graveyard until end of turn.

## 🚀 Future Roadmap: Upgrades & Considerations

### 💡 High-Impact Tech to Consider
*These cards are top-tier candidates for future upgrades or alternative power tuning:*

*   **Survival of the Fittest ({1}{G}):** Top-tier creature tutor that discards creatures to tutor threats directly; would bring the deck's Game Changers to 2/3.
*   **Squandered Resources ({B}{G}):** Sacrifices lands for extra mana, enabling monstrous mana bursts alongside Splendid Reclamation.
*   **Constant Mists ({1}{G}):** Fog with Buyback: sacrifice a land. With Crucible/Necrobloom, creates a soft combat lock.
*   **Field of the Dead (Land):** Redundant second copy of Necrobloom's zombie engine. (Note: Field of the Dead is an official Game Changer; adding it would increase Game Changers to 2/3).
