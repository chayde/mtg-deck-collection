## 1. Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

* **Final Placement:** **Bracket 3**
* **DTI Threat Index:** `40 / 96` (Score Floor: Bracket 3)
* **Clock Speed (Velocity Vector):** `21 / 48`
* **Opponent Stifle (Suppression Vector):** `15 / 40`
* **WotC Statutory Compliance:** 1 / 3 Game Changers (Floor: Bracket 3)
  * *Game Changers detected:* Fierce Guardianship

### Tactical Overview
A Grixis (UBR) Inverted-Triangle upkeep-multiplier deck built around Obeka, Splitter of Seconds. The deck pairs unblockable/haste/double-strike combat enablers (Brotherhood Regalia, Winged Boots, Silent Arbiter, Lizard Blades, and the M13 Upkeep Ring trio) with 26 upkeep and time-counter payoffs (all 6 Grixis Monarch Courts, Rilsa Rael's Initiative/Undercity engine, Extravagant Replication, Mechanized Production, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Twilight Prophet, and Plargg and Nassari). Once Obeka connects in combat around Turn 5-6, she generates 2 to 8+ additional upkeep steps immediately after combat, compounding card advantage, life drain, and token production to close the game within 1 opponent response cycle.

## 2. Operational Primer & Strategy

### Core Strategy
1. Turns 1-3 (Mana, Haste & Evasion Setup): Deploy 2-mana rocks (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talismans, Nightscape Familiar) and pre-drop a haste or unblockable equipment/aura (Swiftfoot Boots, Lightning Greaves, Ring of Valkas, Brotherhood Regalia, Winged Boots, or Trailblazer's Boots) or suspend early spells (Ancestral Vision, Profane Tutor).
2. Turns 4-5 (Commander Deployment & First Upkeep Burst): Cast Obeka, Splitter of Seconds with haste or protection backup (Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Slip Out the Back) alongside a premier upkeep engine (Rilsa Rael, Kingpin, a Monarch Court, As Foretold, or Bonehoard Dracosaur).
3. Turns 6-7 (Post-Combat Upkeep Avalanche): Connect with an unblockable or double-striking Obeka to trigger 3-8 additional upkeep steps immediately after combat—completing the entire Undercity dungeon in one turn, draining the table for 18+ life via Court of Ambition / Court of Ire / Creeping Bloodsucker / Twilight Prophet, or reaching 8 artifacts with Mechanized Production.

### Mulligan Priorities
* **Keep:** Hands with 3+ lands covering {U}{B}{R}, at least 1 two-mana ramp piece, and 1 haste/evasion enabler or low-curve upkeep engine.
* **Avoid:** 2-land hands without a 2-mana rock (the 35-land baseline can stall on Turn 4 land drops) or top-heavy hands overloaded with 5-7 CMC upkeep payoffs and no evasion for Obeka.

### Tactical Tips
- Silent Arbiter makes Obeka 100% unblockable because Obeka has native Menace (cannot be blocked except by 2+ creatures) while Silent Arbiter limits blockers to at most 1 creature.
- Sequence your M13 Rings (Ring of Valkas, Ring of Xathrid, Ring of Evos Isle) before attaching Shroud equipment (Lightning Greaves or Whispersilk Cloak); Obeka is all three colors (UBR), so every Ring puts a +1/+1 counter on her during every post-combat upkeep.
- Alexios, Deimos of Kosmos triggers on EVERY player's upkeep—meaning after Alexios and Obeka attack on your turn, your post-combat extra upkeeps untap Alexios and stack multiple +1/+1 counters on him before passing to the next opponent.

## 3. Strategic Weaknesses & Exploits

### Critical Vulnerabilities
- 🔴 Commander Removal Prior to Combat Damage: Nearly all explosive turns require Obeka to deal combat damage to a player; instant-speed removal or edicts before the combat damage step cut off the extra upkeep chain.
- 🔴 35-Land Baseline Bottleneck & Filter Signet Friction: Running 35 lands with a 4-CMC 3-color commander and equipment equip costs creates occasional Turn 4-5 mana/color stalls (18.8% of seats delayed past Turn 5 in Goldfish testing).

### Moderate Friction Points
- 🟡 Shroud Self-Targeting Conflict: Attaching Whispersilk Cloak or Lightning Greaves prevents you from equipping additional Rings, Lizard Blades, or Aqueous Form onto Obeka unless a second creature is available to temporarily hold the Shroud equipment.
- 🟡 Fog / Maze of Ith Effects: Preventing Obeka's combat damage (without destroying her) neutralizes her upkeep trigger for the turn.

### Minor Exploits
- ⚪ Monarch / Initiative Theft by Flying Swarm Decks: If Silent Arbiter is not on the battlefield, evasive token decks can steal the Monarch or Initiative crown before your turn, halving the output of your Courts.

## 4. Key Engine Anchors

- **[Obeka, Splitter of Seconds](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Obeka%2C%20Splitter%20of%20Seconds%22):** Command-zone upkeep multiplier; grants X additional upkeep steps immediately after combat equal to combat damage dealt.
- **[Rilsa Rael, Kingpin](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Rilsa%20Rael%2C%20Kingpin%22):** Introduces the Initiative (Undercity ventures on every upkeep); 4 upkeeps complete the entire Undercity in one combat, then Rilsa grants Obeka +5/+0, first strike, menace, and deathtouch.
- **[Silent Arbiter](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Silent%20Arbiter%22):** Combines with Obeka's native Menace to make Obeka 100% unblockable while preventing opponents from attacking with more than 1 creature to steal Monarch/Initiative.
- **[Lizard Blades](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Lizard%20Blades%22):** Grants Double Strike, splitting Obeka's damage across First Strike and Regular Combat Damage steps to double her post-combat upkeep triggers.
- **[Ring of Valkas](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Ring%20of%20Valkas%22):** Grants Haste and puts a +1/+1 counter on Obeka at the start of every upkeep step, compounding her power mid-turn.
- **[Court of Vantress](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Court%20of%20Vantress%22):** Introduces Monarch and creates token copies of your best artifacts or enchantments (Paradox Haze, other Courts, or Rings) on every upkeep.
- **[Court of Ambition](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Court%20of%20Ambition%22):** Forces each opponent to discard 2 cards or lose 6 life on every upkeep while you are the Monarch.
- **[Bonehoard Dracosaur](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Bonehoard%20Dracosaur%22):** Exiles 2 cards per upkeep to play and generates 3/1 Dinosaurs and Treasure tokens across every post-combat upkeep.
- **[Fierce Guardianship](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Fierce%20Guardianship%22):** Sole Game Changer (1/3); free counterspell to protect Obeka during the critical combat step.
- **[Deflecting Swat](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!%22Deflecting%20Swat%22):** Free redirection shield to turn opposing targeted removal away from Obeka.
