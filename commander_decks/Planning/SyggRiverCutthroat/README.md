## Strategy Summary

Sygg sits in the command zone as an omnipresent card draw engine that rewards any turn in which an opponent loses 3 or more life. This deck operates as a hybrid between **Dimir Group Slug** and **Goad Puppet-Mastery**:

1. **Passive Life Drains:** Enchantments like **Painful Quandary**, **Breathstealer's Crypt**, **Bloodchief Ascension**, and **Vile Consumption** punish opponents for casting spells, drawing cards, and holding creatures, steadily tripping Sygg's 3-damage threshold.
2. **Forced Combat:** The **Impetus Aura Suite** (**Coercive**, **Ghoulish**, **Parasitic**, **Psychic Impetus**, and **Eye of Nidhogg**) forces opponents' strongest threats to beat each other down, protecting your life total while generating end-step card advantage on every turn cycle.
3. **Instant-Speed Control:** Backed by **Mana Drain**, **Deadly Rollick**, **Bender's Waterskin** (which untaps on every player's turn), and sweepers like **Cyclonic Rift**, **Damnation**, and **Toxrill, the Corrosive**, the deck maintains control over the stack and board while steadily grinding out the table.
