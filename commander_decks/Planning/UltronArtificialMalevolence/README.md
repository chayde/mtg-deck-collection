## Strategic Summary
Operates as a **Colorless Token Duplication & Robot Swarm Engine**. Ultron turns every nontoken artifact entering the battlefield into a duplication factory by paying {2}. If the copied artifact is non-creature (mana rocks, equipment, utility artifacts), the token enters as a **2/2 Robot Villain artifact creature**, immediately creating an aggressive board presence while keeping all printed abilities.

Key synergies include:
*   **The Bobblehead Snowball:** Duplicating any of the 7 Fallout Bobbleheads accelerates their $X$-scaling abilities exponentially.
*   **Robot Multipliers:** [Roaming Throne](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Roaming+Throne") ({4}) naming Robot doubles Ultron's copy triggers, allowing double payments for two token copies per entering artifact.
*   **Robot Swarm Overrun:** [Cybermen Squadron](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cybermen+Squadron") ({7}) gives nonlegendary artifact creatures Myriad, while [Krang, Utrom Warlord](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Krang,+Utrom+Warlord") ({9}) grants the entire artifact army flying, trample, indestructible, and haste.
*   **Sacrifice & Recursion Loops:** [Krark-Clan Ironworks](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Krark-Clan+Ironworks") ({4}) and [Ashnod's Altar](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ashnod's+Altar") ({3}) feed into [Scrap Trawler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Scrap+Trawler") ({3}), [Myr Retriever](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Myr+Retriever") ({2}), and [Junk Diver](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Junk+Diver") ({3}) loops for lethal [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Aetherflux+Reservoir") ({4}) bursts or [Walking Ballista](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Walking+Ballista") ({X}{X}) finishes.
