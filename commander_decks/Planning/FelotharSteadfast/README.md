## Strategy Summary

Felothar sits in the command zone as an Abzan engine that unlocks defenders, converts massive toughness into lethal combat damage, and serves as a burst draw battery:

1. **Defender Swarm & Combat Punch:** Low-cost walls and high-toughness creatures like **Wall of Omens**, **Carven Caryatid**, **Shield Sphere**, and **Indomitable Ancients** attack early and hit with full toughness power.
2. **Team Vigilance & Unshakable Defense:** **Brave the Sands**, **Reconnaissance**, **Oathsworn Giant**, and **Sight of the Scalelords** ensure your wall army attacks aggressively while remaining available to block, backed by **Perimeter Captain**'s life gain.
3. **One-Sided Power Sweepers:** **Wave of Reckoning**, **Slaughter the Strong**, and **Dusk // Dawn** wipe out opponent boards while leaving your low-power, high-toughness creatures completely unharmed.
4. **Reach & Fling Finishers:** **Catapult Fodder // Catapult Captain** flings high-toughness creatures directly at opponents, **Tree of Perdition** sets life totals to 13, and **Tower Defense** provides an instant-speed +5/+5 team overrun.
