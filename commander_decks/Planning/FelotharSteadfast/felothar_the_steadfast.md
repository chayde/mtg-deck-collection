# Deck Guide: Felothar the Steadfast ("The Iron Citadel")

![Felothar the Steadfast](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/2/c/2c3549f6-25df-4ea7-84ad-922ccd4af6b2.jpg?1783907190)

## Commander Strategy
**Felothar the Steadfast** ({1}{W}{B}{G})
*   **Archetype:** Abzan ({W}{B}{G}) Defender Beatdown / Toughness Overrun & Fling Reach
*   **Bracket:** 3 (Upgraded — Casual / Synergy — 0 Game Changers)
*   **Core Goal:** Deploy an ultra-resilient, low-cost army of high-toughness defenders and walls that hit like freight trains using their toughness under Felothar. Maintain an impenetrable defensive shield with team vigilance and double-blocking, wipe opponent boards with one-sided power-based sweepers, and close out games through explosive +0/+5 overruns, unblockable skulk attacks, or direct toughness flings straight to the face.

### The Iron Citadel Engine
1.  **Toughness Combat ("Butt-Strike") & Unlocked Defenders:** Felothar turns defensive walls like *Wall of Omens* (0/4), *Carven Caryatid* (2/5), *Wall of Junk* (0/7), and *Indomitable Ancients* (2/10) into massive early-game attackers. Redundancies (*Doran, the Siege Tower*, *Ancient Lumberknot*, *Bedrock Tortoise*, *Assault Formation*, *Rasaad yn Bashir*) ensure your army continues hitting at full power even if Felothar is removed.
2.  **Permanent Shield (Vigilance & Double Blocking):** Through *Brave the Sands*, *Reconnaissance*, *Oathsworn Giant*, and *Sight of the Scalelords*, your wall army attacks aggressively while remaining completely untapped and available to block. *Perimeter Captain* rewards blocking with massive life gain.
3.  **Burst Sacrifice Draw Engine:** Felothar's activated ability (`{3}, {T}, Sacrifice another creature: Draw cards equal to toughness, discard cards equal to power`) draws massive handfuls of cards off 0-power defenders (*Tree of Perdition* draws 13 cards, *Shield Sphere* draws 6 cards, *Carven Caryatid* draws 5 cards) with **zero discard penalty**.
4.  **Asymmetric Sweepers:** Because your board features low power (0–2) and high toughness (4–13), sweepers like *Wave of Reckoning*, *Slaughter the Strong*, and *Dusk // Dawn* decimate opponent boards while leaving your entire army completely untouched.
5.  **Reach & Alternate Win Conditions:** If combat stalls, *Catapult Fodder // Catapult Captain* launches high-toughness creatures directly at opponents' life totals, *Tree of Perdition* drops an opponent to 13 life, and *Tower Defense* acts as a 2-mana instant-speed +5/+5 team overrun.

## 🛠️ Card Explanations

### 🛡️ Commander (1 Card)
*   **Felothar the Steadfast:** Command zone engine providing toughness combat, defender attack enablement, and burst card draw via high-toughness sacrifice.

### 🧱 Defender & High-Toughness Army (17 Cards)
*   **Shield Sphere:** 0-mana 0/6 wall that attacks for 6 on Turn 2 or sacrifices to Felothar to draw 6 cards for free.
*   **Perimeter Captain:** 1-mana 0/4 defender that gains 2 life whenever any defender blocks, turning team blocks into massive life swings.
*   **Stalwart Shield-Bearers:** 2-mana 0/3 defender that passively gives all your defenders +0/+2 (increasing combat power and survivability).
*   **Wall of Omens:** 2-mana 0/4 defender that replaces itself on ETB and swings for 4.
*   **Wall of Blossoms:** 2-mana 0/4 defender that draws a card on ETB and attacks for 4.
*   **Carven Caryatid:** 3-mana 2/5 defender that cantrips on entry and hits for 5.
*   **Overgrown Battlement:** 2-mana 0/4 defender that taps for massive green mana equal to your defender count.
*   **Axebane Guardian:** 3-mana 0/3 defender that taps for X mana in any color combination based on defender count.
*   **Wall of Roots:** 2-mana 0/5 defender that produces mana immediately on each turn without tapping.
*   **Crashing Drawbridge:** 2-mana 0/4 defender that taps to grant your entire team haste for surprise attacks.
*   **Weathered Sentinels:** 3-mana 2/5 artifact wall with native vigilance, reach, trample, and indestructible attack triggers.
*   **Wall of Tanglecord:** 2-mana 0/6 artifact defender with an optional {G} reach activation.
*   **Gatecreeper Vine:** 2-mana 0/2 defender that fetches a basic land to hand on entry.
*   **Wall of Mulch:** 2-mana 0/4 defender that can sacrifice any wall for {G} to draw a card in response to removal.
*   **Indomitable Ancients:** 4-mana 2/10 Treefolk that hits for 10 combat damage or sacrifices to draw 10 cards.
*   **Wingmantle Chaplain:** 4-mana 0/3 defender that spawns a 1/1 flying bird token for every defender you control and enter.
*   **Tree of Redemption:** 4-mana 0/13 defender that resets your life total to 13, swings for 13, or draws 13 cards.

### ⚔️ Toughness Damage Redundancies & Combat Finishers (7 Cards)
*   **Doran, the Siege Tower:** 3-mana 0/5 backup commander effect for toughness combat.
*   **Ancient Lumberknot:** 4-mana 1/4 backup toughness combat enabler for all creatures with toughness > power.
*   **Bedrock Tortoise:** 4-mana 0/6 that grants hexproof to your creatures on your turn and enables toughness combat.
*   **Rasaad yn Bashir:** 3-mana 0/3 that enables toughness damage and doubles team toughness when attacking with the initiative.
*   **Walking Bulwark:** 1-mana 0/3 defender that gives another defender haste and toughness combat activation.
*   **Assault Formation:** 2-mana enchantment providing toughness combat backup, defender attack unlocking, and team toughness pumping.
*   **Tower Defense:** 2-mana instant that grants +0/+5 and reach to your whole board — functions as an instant +5/+5 team Overrun.

### 👁️ Vigilance & Defensive Fortification (4 Cards)
*   **Brave the Sands:** 2-mana enchantment granting your entire team vigilance and the ability to block an additional creature each combat.
*   **Reconnaissance:** 1-mana enchantment allowing you to untap all attacking creatures after combat damage during the end-of-combat step (pseudo-vigilance + total combat safety).
*   **Sight of the Scalelords:** 5-mana enchantment that gives all creatures with toughness 4+ a +2/+2 boost and vigilance at combat.
*   **Oathsworn Giant:** 6-mana 3/4 Giant that passively buffs other creatures with +0/+2 and vigilance.

### 🎯 Reach & Alternate Win Conditions (4 Cards)
*   **Catapult Fodder // Catapult Captain:** 3-mana zombie that taps and sacrifices a creature to deal direct damage equal to its toughness to target opponent (or each opponent when flipped).
*   **Tree of Perdition:** 4-mana 0/13 defender that sets an opponent's life total to 13, swings for 13, or sacrifices to Felothar to draw 13 cards.
*   **Stoneskin:** 3-mana flash aura providing +0/+10 — creates a surprise 10-damage lethal blocker/attacker or draws 10 cards with Felothar.
*   **Behind the Scenes:** 3-mana enchantment granting Skulk, rendering 0-power defenders completely unblockable.

### 📚 Card Advantage & Velocity (8 Cards)
*   **Welcoming Vampire:** Draws a card whenever a power 2 or less creature enters your battlefield (once per turn).
*   **Tocasia's Welcome:** Draws a card whenever a low mana value creature enters the battlefield (once per turn).
*   **Mentor of the Meek:** Triggers on low-power creature entry to draw cards for {1}.
*   **Beast Whisperer:** Draws a card on every creature spell cast.
*   **Morbid Opportunist:** Draws a card whenever any creature dies (once per turn, synergizing with Felothar sacrifices).
*   **Painful Truths:** 3-mana sorcery that draws 3 cards for 3 life in 3 colors.
*   **Night's Whisper:** Efficient 2-mana draw 2 cards.
*   **Harmonize:** 4-mana sorcery that draws 3 cards.

### ⚡ Ramp & Mana Acceleration (8 Cards)
*   **Sol Ring:** Premier colorless acceleration.
*   **Arcane Signet:** 2-mana color fixing for Abzan.
*   **Fellwar Stone:** 2-mana rock tapping for opponents' colors.
*   **Golgari Signet:** 2-mana Golgari mana filter and acceleration.
*   **Orzhov Signet:** 2-mana Orzhov mana filter and acceleration.
*   **Selesnya Signet:** 2-mana Selesnya mana filter and acceleration.
*   **Nature's Lore:** Fetches an untapped Forest dual (Overgrown Tomb / Temple Garden / Murmuring Bosk).
*   **Farseek:** Fetches any dual land with basic land types Plains, Swamp, or Mountain/Forest.

### 🗡️ Targeted Removal & Protection (11 Cards)
*   **Swords to Plowshares:** 1-mana instant creature exile.
*   **Path to Exile:** 1-mana instant creature exile.
*   **Assassin's Trophy:** 2-mana instant unconditional permanent destruction.
*   **Anguished Unmaking:** 3-mana instant nonland permanent exile.
*   **Tear Asunder:** Flexible artifact/enchantment exile with kicker for nonland permanent exile.
*   **Beast Within:** Unconditional permanent destruction into a 3/3 beast token.
*   **Generous Gift:** Unconditional permanent destruction into a 3/3 elephant token.
*   **Stroke of Midnight:** 3-mana instant nonland permanent destruction into a 1/1 token.
*   **Heroic Intervention:** Instant team hexproof and indestructible protection.
*   **Flawless Maneuver:** Free indestructible protection for your team while controlling Felothar.
*   **Zetalpa, Primal Dawn:** Indestructible, vigilant, flying 4/8 dinosaur beater and permanent blocker that hits for 8 double-strike.

### 💥 Asymmetrical Mass Disruption (4 Cards)
*   **Wave of Reckoning:** 5-mana sorcery where each creature deals damage to itself equal to its power; your 0-power defenders take 0 damage, wiping opponent boards.
*   **Slaughter the Strong:** 3-mana sorcery where each player keeps total power 4 or less; preserves your entire board while wiping opponent armies.
*   **Dusk // Dawn:** 4-mana wipe for power 3+, with Aftermath {3}{W}{W} to return all power 2 or less creatures from graveyard to hand.
*   **Bojuka Bog:** Targeted graveyard hate on a land.

### 🌍 Lands (37 Cards)
*   **Command Tower:** 3-color untapped land.
*   **Overgrown Tomb / Temple Garden / Godless Shrine:** Shock land duals with basic types.
*   **Undergrowth Stadium / Vault of Champions / Bountiful Promenade:** Multiplayer untapped crowd duals.
*   **Llanowar Wastes / Caves of Koilos / Brushland:** Untapped pain lands.
*   **Sandsteppe Citadel:** 3-color tri-land.
*   **Murmuring Bosk:** Fetchable Forest that taps for Abzan colors.
*   **Exotic Orchard:** Multiplayer rainbow land.
*   **Fabled Passage:** Landfall color fixing and deck thinning.
*   **Rogue's Passage:** Unblockable utility land.
*   **Reliquary Tower:** Unlimited hand size for large Felothar draw turns.
*   **Bojuka Bog:** Graveyard exile on a land.
*   **7 Plains, 6 Swamp, 7 Forest:** Balanced basic land suite.
