# Deck Guide: Gisa, the Hellraiser — "The Hellraiser's Horde"

![Gisa, the Hellraiser](https://cards.scryfall.io/normal/front/d/b/db7c078b-0325-473f-8097-4dda73264139.jpg?1712355592)

## 🧟 Commander Strategy

**Gisa, the Hellraiser** ({3}{B}{B}) is an overwhelming 4/4 Zombie Warlock powerhouse that solves the single biggest bottleneck of casual Zombie typal decks: **relying on slow, clunky token generators that require untapping or expensive mana sinks.**

Gisa combines three elite abilities into a single commander:
1. **Inherent Protection:** `Ward—{2}, Pay 2 life` punishes early single-target removal and makes tempo trades miserable for opponents.
2. **Built-in Anthem & Menace Evasion:** All Skeletons and Zombies you control get `+1/+1 and have menace`. This immediately transforms vanilla 2/2 Zombie tokens into terrifying 3/3 menacing attackers that force 2-for-1 blocks across the table.
3. **The Crime Swarm Spigot (CR 700.13):** Whenever you commit a crime, create two 2/2 black Zombie Rogue creature tokens. This ability triggers **once each turn**.

### 🔍 Mastering the Crime Engine (CR 700.13)
Under Magic Comprehensive Rules, **committing a crime** occurs whenever you cast a spell, activate an ability, or put a triggered ability on the stack that targets at least one of the following:
* An opponent
* A permanent an opponent controls
* A spell an opponent controls
* A card in an opponent's graveyard

Because Gisa's trigger specifies **"once each turn"** (not "once during your turn"), an instant-speed crime committed on an opponent's turn triggers Gisa. In a standard 4-player pod:
* **Your Turn:** Commit a crime $\rightarrow$ create two 3/3 menacing Zombie Rogues.
* **Opponent 1's Turn:** Commit a crime at instant speed $\rightarrow$ create two 3/3 menacing Zombie Rogues.
* **Opponent 2's Turn:** Commit a crime at instant speed $\rightarrow$ create two 3/3 menacing Zombie Rogues.
* **Opponent 3's Turn:** Commit a crime at instant speed $\rightarrow$ create two 3/3 menacing Zombie Rogues.

**Result:** A full round rotation generates **8 Zombie Rogues (24 power with menace)** without casting another creature spell!

### ⚙️ Proactive, Zero-Mana & Repeatable Crime Engines
Rather than relying purely on reactive removal spells that empty your hand, this deck runs premier repeatable and proactive crime triggers:
* **[Zombie Trailblazer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zombie+Trailblazer") ({B}{B}{B}):** Tap an untapped Zombie you control to target an opponent's land (makes it a Swamp until end of turn) or an opponent's creature (gives it swampwalk). **Costs {0} mana at instant speed!** Furthermore, tapping a summoning-sick Zombie Rogue that just entered pays the cost immediately (CR 302.6).
* **[Withered Wretch](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Withered+Wretch") ({B}{B}):** `{1}: Exile target card from a graveyard.` Instant-speed, repeatable graveyard hate that commits a crime whenever targeting an opponent's graveyard card.
* **[Cemetery Reaper](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cemetery+Reaper") ({1}{B}{B}):** `{2}{B}, {T}: Exile target creature card from a graveyard. Create a 2/2 black Zombie creature token.` Targeting an opponent's graveyard creates 1 Zombie from the Reaper PLUS 2 Zombie Rogues from Gisa = **3 Zombies in one activation!**
* **[Ghost Vacuum](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ghost+Vacuum") ({1}) & [Relic of Progenitus](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Relic+of+Progenitus") ({1}):** Tap to exile target card from an opponent's graveyard for {0} mana at instant speed.
* **[Agatha's Soul Cauldron](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Agatha's+Soul+Cauldron") ({2}):** Tap to exile a creature card from an opponent's graveyard at instant speed, distributing +1/+1 counters to your army and stealing activated abilities.
* **[Demolition Field](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Demolition+Field") & [Scavenger Grounds](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Scavenger+Grounds"):** Land-based utility crimes that disrupt opponent lands or graveyards while advancing your board.

### 🏆 Core Win Conditions & Alternate Finishers

1. **The Menace Horde Overrun:** Swarming the board with 10–25 Zombies buffed by typal anthems ([Death Baron](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Death+Baron"), [Lord of the Undead](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Lord+of+the+Undead"), [Lord of the Accursed](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Lord+of+the+Accursed"), [Undead Warchief](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Undead+Warchief"), [Cemetery Reaper](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cemetery+Reaper")) and typal multipliers ([Coat of Arms](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Coat+of+Arms"), [Banner of Kinship](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Banner+of+Kinship"), [Eldrazi Monument](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Eldrazi+Monument")). Because Gisa gives all Zombies menace, opponents cannot afford to block without throwing away their entire board.
2. **Unblockable Swampwalk (Zombie Master + Urborg):** Assembling [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Urborg,+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth") alongside [Zombie Master](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zombie+Master") turns all lands on the board into Swamps, making your entire Zombie army completely **unblockable** and granting `{B}: Regenerate this creature`.
3. **The Noxious Ghoul Asymmetric Wipeout:** Whenever [Noxious Ghoul](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Noxious+Ghoul") ({3}{B}{B}) or another Zombie enters, all non-Zombie creatures get -1/-1 until end of turn. Whenever Gisa commits a crime, two Zombies enter, giving all non-Zombies -2/-2. Committing 2–3 crimes or casting mass token spells permanently annihilates all opposing armies while leaving your Zombies untouched!
4. **Bringer of the Last Gift ({6}{B}{B}):** Symmetrical creature wipe and asymmetric mass reanimation. Sacrificing your board wipes all other creatures, then immediately reanimates every creature from your graveyard with haste or menace.
5. **Mikaeus, the Unhallowed ({3}{B}{B}{B}):** Gives all non-Humans +1/+1 and **Undying**, rendering your entire Zombie army immune to opposing board wipes. Any wipe returns your army bigger and triggers all ETBs again!
6. **Army of the Damned ({5}{B}{B}{B}):** Vomits thirteen 2/2 black Zombie tokens onto the battlefield (thirteen 3/3 menace Zombies under Gisa = 39 power of evasion attackers) with Flashback to do it again from the graveyard!
7. **Yawgmoth, Thran Physician ({2}{B}{B}):** Sacrifices Zombie tokens for {0} mana at instant speed to place -1/-1 counters on opponent creatures (commits crimes!), drawing cards while Gisa spawns 2 new Zombies per rotation.
8. **Gray Merchant of Asphodel ({3}{B}{B}):** Drains each opponent for life equal to your devotion to black and gains that much life. Devastating when mass-reanimated via *Bringer of the Last Gift*, *Living Death*, or *Patriarch's Bidding*.

### 🚜 Piloting the Engine (Turn-by-Turn Blueprint)

* **Turns 1–3 (Setup & Ramp):** Establish mana acceleration ([Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sol+Ring"), [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Arcane+Signet"), [Jet Medallion](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Jet+Medallion"), [Liquimetal Torque](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Liquimetal+Torque"), [Wayfarer's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Wayfarer's+Bauble"), [Expedition Map](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Expedition+Map")). Deploy low-curve engine pieces ([Cryptbreaker](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cryptbreaker"), [Forsaken Miner](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Forsaken+Miner"), [Ghost Vacuum](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ghost+Vacuum"), [Withered Wretch](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Withered+Wretch"), [Unlicensed Hearse](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Unlicensed+Hearse")).
* **Turn 4 (Deploy Value Engine or Commander):** Cast [Black Market Connections](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Black+Market+Connections"), [The One Ring](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"The+One+Ring"), or cast [Gisa, the Hellraiser](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Gisa,+the+Hellraiser") with protection or immediate crime open. Equip [Commander's Plate](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Commander's+Plate") to grant Gisa protection from White, Blue, Red, and Green!
* **Turns 5–6 (The Crime Rotation):** Maintain open mana or an active tap ability ([Zombie Trailblazer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zombie+Trailblazer"), [Ghost Vacuum](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ghost+Vacuum"), [Agatha's Soul Cauldron](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Agatha's+Soul+Cauldron"), [Unlicensed Hearse](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Unlicensed+Hearse"), [Liquimetal Torque](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Liquimetal+Torque"), [Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Deserted+Temple"), [Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Yawgmoth,+Thran+Physician")). Commit crimes on each player's turn to generate 6–8 menacing Zombies per rotation.
* **Turn 7+ (Big Mana & Apocalypse):** Tap [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cabal+Coffers") + [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Urborg,+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth"), [Three Tree City](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Three+Tree+City"), or [Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nykthos,+Shrine+to+Nyx") for 15–30 mana. Resolve [Coat of Arms](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Coat+of+Arms"), [Banner of Kinship](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Banner+of+Kinship"), [Bolas's Citadel](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bolas's+Citadel"), or [Army of the Damned](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Army+of+the+Damned") to close out the game in an overwhelming alpha strike.
