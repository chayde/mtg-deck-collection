Tactical Overview

This mono-black deck is led by Gisa, the Hellraiser and focuses on swarming the board with evasive Zombie tokens generated through the crime mechanic. The deck uses cheap, repeatable graveyard exile artifacts like Agatha's Soul Cauldron and Ghost Vacuum alongside low-cost removal spells to trigger Gisa on both your turn and opponents' turns. As tokens multiply, tribal lords such as Undead Warchief and Death Baron amplify their power, while Zombie Master paired with Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth grants unblockable swampwalk. Draw engines like Undead Augur and Species Specialist keep resources flowing, and powerful finishers including Gray Merchant of Asphodel, Coat of Arms, and Living Death close out the game.

Operational Primer & Strategy

Core Turn Strategy

  1. Deploy low-cost mana ramp and repeatable crime enablers such as Ghost Vacuum or Agatha's Soul Cauldron in the early turns.
  2. Cast Gisa, the Hellraiser on turn 4 or 5 and immediately trigger a crime to create two 3/3 menace Zombie tokens.
  3. Target opposing graveyards or creatures on each opponent's turn to maximize Gisa's once-per-turn token generation.
  4. Overwhelm the table with anthem-buffed menace attackers, unblockable swampwalk attacks, or massive devotion drain from Gray Merchant of Asphodel.

Mulligan Priorities

Keep: ['Hands containing 3 lands, at least one 2-CMC ramp piece, and a cheap repeatable crime enabler or card draw engine.']

Avoid: ['Hands filled with expensive 5+ mana spells, lacking early black mana sources, or without any proactive crime enablers.']

Tactical Tips

  • Activate graveyard exile abilities on opponents' turns to generate additional Zombie tokens outside of your own turn.
  • Combine Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth with Zombie Master or Zombie Trailblazer to make your entire army unblockable against all players.
  • Hold mass reanimation spells like Living Death or Patriarch's Bidding in hand to instantly rebuild your board following an opponent's sweeper.

Strategic Weaknesses & Interaction Exploits

Critical Vulnerabilities

  • Vulnerability to exile-based board wipes like Farewell that bypass graveyard recursion and dismantle wide token boards.

Moderate Friction Points

  • Lack of stack interaction leaves the deck unable to disrupt noncreature combo wins.
  • High reliance on Gisa, the Hellraiser to achieve format-competitive token generation speed.

Minor Weaknesses

  • Substantial life payments from card draw engines and removal spells can reduce your life buffer against aggressive strategies.
