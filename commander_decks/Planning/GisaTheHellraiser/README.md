## Strategic Summary
Operates as an overwhelming **Mono-Black Zombie Swarm Engine**. Commanded by **Gisa, the Hellraiser** ({3}{B}{B}), the deck solves the traditional weaknesses of zombie typal lists (vulnerability to board wipes, slow token generation, and creature stalling) by weaponizing **CR 700.13 Crime Triggers**:

1. **Instant-Speed Crime Triggers (8 Zombies per Round):** Gisa triggers "once each turn" whenever you commit a crime, creating two 2/2 Zombie Rogues. By utilizing instant-speed, proactive crime enablers ([Zombie Trailblazer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zombie+Trailblazer"), [Withered Wretch](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Withered+Wretch"), [Cemetery Reaper](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cemetery+Reaper"), [Ghost Vacuum](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ghost+Vacuum"), [Relic of Progenitus](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Relic+of+Progenitus"), [Agatha's Soul Cauldron](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Agatha's+Soul+Cauldron")), the deck commits crimes on every opponent's turn, vomiting up to **8 Zombie Rogues (24 power with menace)** per turn rotation!
2. **Innate Menace & Typal Multipliers:** Gisa grants all Skeletons and Zombies `+1/+1 and menace`. Supported by premier typal lords ([Death Baron](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Death+Baron"), [Undead Warchief](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Undead+Warchief"), [Lord of the Undead](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Lord+of+the+Undead")) and colorless multipliers ([Coat of Arms](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Coat+of+Arms"), [Banner of Kinship](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Banner+of+Kinship"), [Eldrazi Monument](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Eldrazi+Monument"), [Roaming Throne](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Roaming+Throne")), the horde rapidly scales into unblockable, lethal combat damage.
3. **Alternate Win Conditions & Asymmetric Wipes:** 
   - [Bringer of the Last Gift](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bringer+of+the+Last+Gift") wipes all other creatures and mass-reanimates your entire graveyard.
   - [Mikaeus, the Unhallowed](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mikaeus,+the+Unhallowed") grants Undying and +1/+1 to your army, guaranteeing resilience against all wipes.
   - [Noxious Ghoul](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Noxious+Ghoul") gives all non-Zombies -1/-1 per entering Zombie, turning Gisa's crime triggers into recurring one-sided board wipes.
   - [Zombie Master](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zombie+Master") + [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Urborg,+Tomb+of+Yawgmoth") provides unblockable Swampwalk.
   - [Acererak the Archlich](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Acererak+the+Archlich") loops dungeons for drain, draw, and treasure.
4. **The Big Mana Mono-Black Engine:** Cabal Coffers + Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth, Cabal Stronghold, Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx, Three Tree City, and Crypt Ghast generate 15–30+ mana to power out game-ending spells.
