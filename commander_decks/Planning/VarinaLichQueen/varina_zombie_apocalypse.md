# Deck Guide: Varina, Lich Queen (Esper Zombie Apocalypse)

## Commander Strategy
**[Varina, Lich Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/291/varina-lich-queen?utm_source=api)** ({1}{W}{U}{B})
*   **Archetype:** Esper ({W}{U}{B}) Go-Wide Zombie Swarm / Card Velocity & Combat Overrun
*   **Bracket:** 3 (Upgraded Casual — Winning Turns 6–8)
*   **Game Changers (3/3):** [Field of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/247/field-of-the-dead?utm_source=api), [Teferi's Protection](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/32/teferis-protection?utm_source=api), [Fierce Guardianship](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/94/fierce-guardianship?utm_source=api)
*   **Core Goal:** Deploy a relentless swarm of decaying, flying, and pumped zombie tokens to overwhelm opponents in mass combat. Strictly avoids aristocrat death-drain loops and slow graveyard recursion in favor of aggressive token production, astronomical card velocity, and unblockable combat alpha strikes.

---

### The 3 Strategic Pillars of the Deck

#### 1. Astronomical Card Velocity
Varina does not rely on top-deck luck. Every time zombies attack, her trigger loots X cards (draw X, discard X) and gains X life. Attacking with even 3–5 zombies digs deep into the library, allowing you to sculpt a hand full of game-ending token doublers, lord anthems, and free protection spells while filling the graveyard with fodder.

#### 2. The Command-Zone Token Spigot
While many players mistake Varina as merely a card filter, her second ability—`{2}, Exile two cards from your graveyard: Create a tapped 2/2 black Zombie creature token`—turns every discarded land or utility spell into immediate board presence. At the end of opponents' turns right before untapping, unspent mana is converted into instant-speed zombies to attack on your next turn.

#### 3. Overcoming the "Vanilla 2/2 Blocked" Hurdle
Vanilla 2/2 zombies stall out on cluttered boards. This deck solves evasion decisively through blue and white:
*   **Permanent Flying:** Discarding [Wonder](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/170/wonder?utm_source=api) to Varina instantly gives the entire army flying as long as an Island is in play.
*   **Swampwalk Lockout:** [Zombie Master](https://scryfall.com/card/me4/105/zombie-master?utm_source=api) combined with [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/257/urborg-tomb-of-yawgmoth?utm_source=api) renders the entire horde unblockable across all opponents.
*   **Lethal Alpha Strikes:** [Akroma's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/125/akromas-will?utm_source=api) closes games out of nowhere with double strike, flying, lifelink, indestructible, and protection from all colors.
