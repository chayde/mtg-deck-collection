## Strategic Summary
Engineered as an overwhelming **Go-Wide Zombie Apocalypse** deck that completely rejects aristocrat/drain loops and infinite graveyard recursion in favor of raw combat mass and card velocity. 

Varina's attack trigger (*"draw X, discard X, gain X life"*) filters through 30–50 cards of the library each game, digging directly into game-ending token multipliers ([Anointed Procession](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/2/anointed-procession?utm_source=api), [Mondrak, Glory Dominus](https://scryfall.com/card/one/23/mondrak-glory-dominus?utm_source=api), [Necroduality](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/79/necroduality?utm_source=api), and [Tombstone Stairwell](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/149/tombstone-stairwell?utm_source=api)). 

Discarded creatures instantly empower [Wonder](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/170/wonder?utm_source=api) to give the entire horde permanent Flying evasion, while unspent mana is converted into instant-speed 2/2 Zombies via Varina's command-zone activated ability. The horde is protected from board sweepers via free phase-outs ([Clever Concealment](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/125/clever-concealment?utm_source=api), [Teferi's Protection](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/32/teferis-protection?utm_source=api)) and finishes games decisively with [Akroma's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/125/akromas-will?utm_source=api).
