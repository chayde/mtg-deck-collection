## ⚙️ Core Mathematical Strategy
1. **Turn 2 Henzie Deployment:** Powered by 8 one-mana mana dorks (*Birds of Paradise*, *Delighted Halfling*, *Ignoble Hierarch*, *Elves of Deep Shadow*, *Llanowar Elves*, *Fyndhorn Elves*, *Elvish Mystic*, *Orcish Lumberjack*).
2. **Velocity & Sac Loops:** Continuously blitzing 4–6 CMC threats with haste and death draws, sacrificed into *Birthing Ritual*, *Greater Good*, or *Industrial Advancement*.
3. **Overwhelming Non-Combo Finishers:** Closing games via *Living Death*, *Terror of the Peaks*, *Moraug, Fury of Akoum*, *Archon of Cruelty*, and *Apex Devastator*.
