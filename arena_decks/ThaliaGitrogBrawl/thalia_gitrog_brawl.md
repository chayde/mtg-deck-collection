# Thalia and The Gitrog Monster — MTG Arena Brawl Guide

## Commander Strategy
* **Commander:** [**Thalia and The Gitrog Monster**]({1}{W}{B}{G})(https://scryfall.com/card/mom/255/thalia-and-the-gitrog-monster?utm_source=api)
* **Format:** MTG Arena 100-Card Brawl
* **Archetype:** Abzan Landfall, Stax & Graveyard Midrange

### Core Game Plan
1. **Asymmetric Tempo Drag (The Thalia Half):** Thalia and The Gitrog Monster forces all opposing creatures and nonbasic lands to enter the battlefield tapped. This severely blunts enemy aggro, breaks haste lines, and forces opponent mana bases to lag an entire turn behind.
2. **Landfall Engine & Resource Acceleration (The Gitrog Half):** Grants an additional land drop every single turn. Whenever she attacks, you sacrifice a land or creature to draw a card, triggering graveyard synergies while refilling your grip.
3. **Explosive Recursion & Swarm Finishers:** Utilize *Crucible of Worlds*, *Ramunap Excavator*, *Ancient Greenwarden*, and *The Necrobloom* to continuously replay sacrificed lands and fetchlands from the graveyard, culminating in massive game-ending bursts with *Lumra, Bellow of the Woods*, *Scute Swarm*, *Bristly Bill*, and *Felidar Retreat*.

* [**Thalia and The Gitrog Monster**]({1}{W}{B}{G}) — First strike, deathtouch, additional land drop, opponent tapland/creature tax, and attack-sacrifice card draw engine.

## 2. Landfall Payoffs & Swarm Engines
* [**Lumra, Bellow of the Woods**]({4}{G}{G}) — Premier finisher that enters with power/toughness equal to lands and returns ALL land cards from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped, triggering 5–10 Landfall triggers at once.
* [**The Necrobloom**]({1}{W}{B}{G}) — Abzan *Field of the Dead* engine; creates 0/1 Plants or 2/2 Zombies on every land drop, and gives lands in your graveyard Dredge 2.
* [**Springheart Nantuko**]({1}{G}) — Creates 1/1 Insect sacrifice fodder on every land drop, or can be bestowed to clone your best creatures.
* [**Bristly Bill, Spine Sower**]({1}{G}) — Distributes +1/+1 counters on each land drop, with a {3}{G}{G} ability that doubles all counters across your team.
* [**Scute Swarm**]({2}{G}) — Exponential insect/clone generator once you control 6+ lands.
* [**Felidar Retreat**]({3}{W}) — Grants 2/2 Cat Beasts or +1/+1 counters with vigilance on every land entry.
* [**Rampaging Baloths**]({4}{G}{G}) — Spits out 4/4 Beast tokens on every land drop.
* [**Titania, Protector of Argoth**]({3}{G}{G}) — Creates 5/3 Elemental tokens whenever a land you control goes to the graveyard.
* [**Tireless Provisioner**]({2}{G}) — Creates Treasure or Food on every land drop.
* [**Tireless Tracker**]({2}{G}) — Creates Clue tokens for card draw and grows as Clues are cracked.

---

## 3. Graveyard Land Recursion & Acceleration
* [**Crucible of Worlds**]({3}) — Play lands directly from your graveyard.
* [**Ramunap Excavator**]({2}{G}) — Creature-based Crucible effect.
* [**Ancient Greenwarden**]({4}{G}{G}) — Plays lands from the graveyard and doubles all Landfall triggers.
* [**The Gitrog Monster**]({3}{B}{G}) — Additional land drop, draws cards whenever lands enter the yard, and acts as a massive 6/6 deathtouch beater.
* [**Azusa, Lost but Seeking**]({2}{G}) — Allows two additional land drops each turn.
* [**Knight of the Reliquary**]({1}{G}{W}) — Sacrifices Forests or Plains to tutor any utility land directly onto the battlefield.
* [**Lotus Cobra**]({1}{G}) — Adds one mana of any color on each land drop.
* [**Deathrite Shaman**]({B/G}) — 1-drop planeswalker that exiles lands from graveyards for mana, shocks opponents, and gains life.

* [**Drana and Linvala**]({1}{W}{W}{B}) — Shuts down opposing activated creature abilities while stealing them.
* [**Opposition Agent**]({2}{B}) — Flashes in to hijack opposing library searches and tutors.
* [**Dauthi Voidwalker**]({B}{B}) — One-sided graveyard exile with shadow that casts an exiled spell for free.

* [**Deafening Silence**]({W}) — Limits each player to a single noncreature spell per turn.
* [**Manglehorn**]({2}{G}) — Enters opposing artifacts tapped and destroys an artifact on entry.
* [**Dauntless Dismantler**]({1}{W}) — Taxes opposing artifacts to enter tapped and acts as an asymmetrical artifact wipe.
* [**Kambal, Consul of Allocation**]({1}{W}{B}) — Drains opponents 2 life whenever they cast a noncreature spell.
* [**Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite**]({5}{W}{W}) — One-sided anthem (+2/+2 to your team, -2/-2 to all opponents' creatures).
* [**Painful Quandary**]({3}{B}{B}) — Punishes every opponent spell cast with 5 life or a discard.
* [**Smothering Tithe**]({3}{W}) — Punishes card draw with exponential Treasure generation.
